{\large Imbalanced semiprime representations of even integers}\par
Slava Chan\hfill 10 October 2026
\vspace{3pt}\hrule\vspace{6pt}

\textbf{Current claim.} For every sufficiently large even $N$, the draft claims
at least $H_N/100000$ representations
\[
 N=p+rq,\qquad p,q\text{ prime},\quad r=1\text{ or prime},\quad
 r\le q^{0.74816964}.
\]
Here $H_N=C_NN/\log^2N$, with the singular factor in Section~1.
Distinct primes $p$ are counted once. The general claimed coefficient is
$1.96176252-6.7617368\log(1/b)$ for fixed $1/2<b<1$.
The secondary choice $b=0.74816865$ gives $H_N/1000000$.
No effective threshold for $N$ is supplied.

\textbf{What changed.} The revision supplies a restricted moving-residue
argument for the coefficient families actually used, retaining the
oscillatory phase and charging its loss. It uses a full eleven-term weight
and paired geometric deletion. The old external $1.9728$ coefficient and
its derived branch are not inputs. A separate indexed library contains
151 supporting proof texts, with historical material clearly marked.
The latest refinement uses $\omega(s)\ge11/20$ for $s\ge7/3$ on the same
17,572 paired cells; the $12/5$ candidate is superseded.

\textbf{Reproduce the finite checks.} Use Python 3.11 or later, from the
repository root; no third-party Python package is needed:
\begin{verbatim}
python verification/run_verification.py --work-dir replay-quick
\end{verbatim}
The output directory must be new. The compact replay checks the input
manifest, rational upper/lower ledgers, budgets and logarithm margins,
and all 400 final entries after 430 rounds of 106 finite actions from the
seed, then regenerates the adopted refinement using two interval
implementations. The separate release archive supplies the complete selected
numerical evidence and its inherited-binding audit. Consult each README
for commands, hash manifests and the scope of recorded runs.

\textbf{Review status.} This is an AI-assisted research manuscript for
mathematical scrutiny. Successful finite replays do not validate the whole
analytic proof. The release does not claim fresh regeneration of every
historical quadrature, complete machine formalization, or independent
human peer review. Documented provenance edits remove private information;
they do not alter mathematical coefficients or certificate arrays.
