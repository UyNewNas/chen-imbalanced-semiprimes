1. The result being proved

 (U) the extended third upper tree, its adopted fourth/fifth/sixth changes,
     the above-mu second/third fallbacks, and the 87 eighth domains;

     including all their admitted ordinary descendants;
 (P) the positive switched leaves in those constructions and the current
     paired-cropped U11 family, using (U)'s coefficient on each smooth family.

The resulting error is O_A(T/log(T)^A) for the SAME signed coefficient in
the count and exact mean. There is no additional factorization assumption on
an individual integer modulus, no changing of the density cap, and no claim
for arbitrary arithmetic filters. The conclusion is conditional only on the

the next square, since S_j+2t_(j+1)<=S_(j-1)+3t_j. Upper trees have
odd first exits; ordinary lower trees have even first exits and a legal
initial square. These parities supply all ordinary tail squares.

where b_I=sum_(i in I)t_i. Once j is later than every selected slot,
the left side is exactly S_(j-1)+2t_j. Hence every subsequent residual
full-square test is also the needed selected test. The selected set itself
does not change as smaller primes are appended. For a W licence the first
three slots and its ceiling remain unchanged. Merely checking b_I would
not prove this closure.

5. Endpoint factorization is a uniform coefficient representation

Use local scale x, qscale=log_x T, theta=7/32 and source residue parameter
alpha_src>=qscale. This alpha_src is not alpha_state above. Put
vartheta=qscale*ell_aux+2zeta and D=x^(vartheta-2zeta).
For a medium length U let A=U*x^(-zeta). Set
 v0=2vartheta-1,
 u0=min((2-(2+2theta)vartheta)/(3-4theta),
        (2-2vartheta-alpha_src*theta)/3).
The local three-line tent licence says v0<=b_I<=u0; its middle line
must be retained after local rescaling even though it is dominated at
alpha_src=1. Full and selected tests are applied to ORDERED UPPER endpoints.

If C=D/(A*x^b_I)>=1, fix all selected slots in group 2. Greedily assign
each remaining slot to capacities A,C. Its selected square says its
square is at most the product of the remaining capacities; therefore
at least one accepts it. The resulting fixed group endpoints B1,B2,B3
satisfy B1<=A, B2=x^b_I, B3<=C and
 A^2 B2 B3^2 <= x^(1-4zeta),
 T^theta A B1 B2^5 B3^2 <= x^(2-4zeta),
 A^(1+2theta) B1 B2^(5-2theta) B3^(2+2theta)
                         <= x^(2-(4+4theta)zeta).
If C<1, reassign ALL slots into A,D/A using the full squares. Here
1<D/A<x^b_I. With B3=1 the last two inequalities follow by replacing
x^b_I by D/A; the first follows from A^2 B2<=AD<=x^(23/24-5zeta/2).
No putative integer factor of length below one is introduced.

For W initialize capacities A,x^v0,D/(A*x^v0) using only the first two
slots. One fits x^v0; the other fits one of the remaining capacities
because its square is at most their product x^(1-vartheta-2zeta).
All capacities are at least one. Continue inserting whole slots. At the
first slot p that fits none, append it to group 2. Its new product B is
in (x^v0,x^u0], since p<=p3<=x^(u0-v0). Failure of the first capacity
and the full square imply A*B*c<D. Thus the two residual capacities are
valid; the full-square greedy argument finishes. If no failure occurs,
the capacity product bounds directly give the same three monomials.

These algorithms run on endpoint data once per atom and HB length U,
not on individual primes or observed progression errors. Coordinatewise
reduction preserves the bounds. Their strict licences survive qscale,
alpha_src close to one and the fine inflation in section 3.

6. The exact tensor coefficient, including marked colours and beta

Refine every finite interval label to a common disjoint half-open alphabet.
For a slot list with m_I copies of label I let M=prod_I m_I!. Its raw
prime convolution T_J(n) is exactly M/prod_p nu_p(n)! on its support.
Hence the sorted rough coefficient is 1_squarefree*T_J/M.
For any fixed three-group partition let M_j=prod_I m_(I,j)! and
gamma_j=(convolution of group j)/M_j. Then 0<=gamma_j<=1 on ALL
integers, and the exact unrestricted identity is
 T_J/M = (prod_j M_j/M)*(gamma_1*gamma_2*gamma_3).
The restoring scalar is fixed and <=1. Empty groups are delta_1.

A raw MARKED convolution sigma is different: its multiplicity was already
present in the state. Only ordered RESIDUAL slots receive the denominator
M_res. The combined atom is epsilon*1_squarefree*T_all/M_res, with its
original sign epsilon. Regrouping gives restoring scalar
epsilon*prod_j M_j/M_res, bounded by |epsilon|*M_mark for disjoint
marked/residual supports. Normalizing marked sigma additionally is valid
only if both Phi(sigma) and Theta_Q(sigma) are scaled identically. No
one-sided extra factorial is admitted.

Choose w=T^omega below every marked exponent. For p<w, p divides BT iff
p divides T. The small-prime local factors and excluded primes are
therefore identical for every marked prefix B. Choose ONE beta_T+/- pair
with support below the smallest residual reserve. The same pair works
throughout the fixed finite query graph; it is not a coupled beta(B,d).
The sign/upper-lower rule determines which fixed beta is attached on an
atom. Since small and rough prime universes are disjoint, the factorization
n=n_small*n_rough is unique and |(gamma_3*beta)(n)|<=1.

The original global squarefree restriction remains in the actual count
and density. Remove it only in the analytic tensor expansion. If support
D_*<=T^theta_* with theta_*<1, the discrepancy difference is bounded by
 T^o(1) sum_(l<=D_*, exists p>=w:p^2|l)(T/l+1+T/phi(l))
                   << (T+D_*)T^(-omega+o(1)).
The fixed outer tuple multiplicity is absorbed in T^o(1). The +1 includes
the complete product T; tau(0) is never used. This does not replace the
original coefficient by an unrestricted pointwise sieve weight.

8. Exact scope of this completion

The theorem removes the conditional phrase 'if the actual coefficients
have a fixed separated representation' for these finite policies: the
representation, coloured scalar, uniform beta, endpoint partition,
descendant closure and admissible hierarchy have been constructed above.
Its inputs still include the restricted analytic proposition itself and
the independent pointwise/density/integral results when one passes from
this distribution statement to K, c and b. A certificate hash is provenance,
not a proof of any of those inputs. No numeric K/c/b is changed here.

The three important exclusions are substantive: an arbitrary high-rank
weight below T^.598, an arbitrary prefix-dependent small-prime beta, and
a per-integer factorization do not meet this theorem. The actual common
label construction needs none of them. Historical assertions equating
ell_aux and lambda, dividing every marked factorial, or fixing kappa
before the actual moment orders must be read with the corrections above.
No new hole in the coefficient interface was identified. The main TeX,
earlier artifacts, research-state, formalization and GitHub are unchanged.
