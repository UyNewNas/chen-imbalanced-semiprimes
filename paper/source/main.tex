\begin{abstract}
We study representations of sufficiently large even integers in the form
$N=p+rq$, where $p,q$ are primes and $r$ is either $1$ or a prime, with a
restriction on the relative sizes of $r$ and $q$.  Write
$H_N=C_NN/\log^2N$ with the Chen singular factor $C_N$ defined below.
The construction developed here gives, for each fixed $1/2<b<1$,
\[
 \liminf_{\substack{N\to\infty\\2\mid N}}
 \frac{D_b(N)}{H_N}
 \ge 1.96176252-6.7617368\log(1/b),
\]
where $D_b$ counts distinct prime summands with $r\le q^b$.
In particular the resulting eventual lower bounds are $H_N/100000$ at
$b=0.74816964$ and $H_N/1000000$ at $b=0.74816865$.
The proof combines a positive upper sieve for the excluded ordered
semiprimes, a full eleven-term Chen weight with a paired geometric
deletion, and explicitly selected finite numerical inequalities.  A
further estimate on the same deleted cells uses the elementary bound
$\omega(s)\ge11/20$ for $s\ge7/3$ and a sharper payment for the
discarded saved lower estimators.  A
restricted moving-residue distribution argument retains the oscillatory
phase and is matched to the actual compiled coefficients.  We state its
classical inputs and its scope, and include the complete analytic modules
and a finite-certificate specification.  The present version replaces the
earlier distribution interface; it does not invoke the previously used
external coefficient $1.9728$.  The proof is a research manuscript with
computational certificates, not a formally verified theorem or a report
of external mathematical acceptance.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Reading the complete proof modules}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Reading the complete proof modules}
The following twelve appendices reproduce the frozen consolidation modules in
their original ASCII mathematical notation. Their internal equation numbers
are local to each module. Fixed-width notation such as \texttt{sum},
\texttt{prod}, \texttt{<=} and \texttt{1\_condition} denotes sums, products,
inequalities and indicator functions, respectively. These are complete source
texts, rather than abbreviated extracts. Further cited derivations and finite
records are indexed in the accompanying \path{proof-library/} and verification
packages. Publication copies of those libraries explicitly record any redaction
of private provenance; mathematical text is not silently rewritten.

Statements in the frozen modules about the main manuscript, internal review,
or publication status describe the state when that module was written. They do
not supersede the current scope and reproduction statement in the main text.
Appendices A--J provide the baseline analytic construction and numerical
assembly. The adopted same-mask refinement in Appendices K--L replaces the
baseline lower coefficient and its derived exponents. The old values printed
in the baseline source texts are preserved as provenance, not as the current
selected result. The main text states and proves the combined conclusion.
Likewise, an internal ``completed'' or ``PASS'' label is not external validation.

The references to Suzuki in Appendices~\ref{app:upper}--\ref{app:lower}
mean \cite[Propositions~9.4(i) and 11.8, and Lemma~14.7]{Suzuki}; the bibliography
identifies the dated notes and page locations.

\paragraph{Editorial correction.}
The count ``17772'' in the coefficient and upper modules is an arithmetic
typographical error: the two disjoint families have $6091+11481=17572$ cells.
The selected masks and numerical certificate have not changed. The same
typographical error occurs in the supporting positive-pair improvement note;
the source library records all identified locations.
