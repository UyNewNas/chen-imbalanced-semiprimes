Primary sources for the current spectral correspondence and sieve density
Metadata checked at the linked primary records on 10 October 2026.
These are external sources, not copies distributed by this library.

1. Edgar Assing, Valentin Blomer and Junxian Li,
Uniform Titchmarsh divisor problems, arXiv:2005.13915v1 (28 May 2020).
https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.13915v1
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2005.13915v1
Use: (3.1)–(3.8), Lemmas 3.1 and 3.2, PDF pages 7–11.
The correspondence uses the coprime arithmetic-parameter class only.

2. Alexandru Pascadi,
Large sieve inequalities for exceptional Maass forms and the greatest
prime factor of n^2+1, arXiv:2404.04239v3 (15 January 2026).
Journal reference: Forum of Mathematics, Pi 14 (2026), e8.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.04239v3
https://arxiv.org/html/2404.04239v3
https://doi.org/10.1017/fmp.2026.10025
Use: Theorems A and J; Lemmas D and H; Proposition E. The current
application retains one common interval and additive phase across levels.

3. Sary Drappeau,
Sums of Kloosterman sums in arithmetic progressions, and the error term
in the dispersion method, arXiv:1504.05549v4 (11 December 2016).
Journal reference: Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society
114 (2017), no. 4, 684–732.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.05549v4
https://drappeau.perso.math.cnrs.fr/documents/divtm-siegel.pdf
https://doi.org/10.1112/plms.12022
Use: Lemma 4.3, Proposition 4.7 and Lemmas 4.9–4.10 in the cited
author-hosted version. Formula numbering is version-specific.

4. Matthew P. Young,
Explicit calculations with Eisenstein series,
arXiv:1710.03624v2 (3 November 2017).
https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.03624v2
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1710.03624v2
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jnt.2018.11.007
Use: Proposition 8.2 and the formal continuous-spectrum normalization.

5. James Maynard,
Primes in arithmetic progressions to large moduli I: Fixed residue classes,
arXiv:2006.06572v2 (5 April 2021).
https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.06572v2
https://arxiv.org/html/2006.06572v2
Use: rough cross-coprime grouping (Lemma 13.2) and Poisson-tail distinctions
(Lemma 13.4). The current proof constructs its own Gevrey majorant.

6. James Maynard,
Primes in arithmetic progressions to large moduli II: Well-factorable
estimates, arXiv:2006.07088v1 (12 June 2020).
https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.07088v1
Earlier supporting modules refer to this version. These historical
references do not extend its fixed-residue statements to moving residues.

7. Yuta Suzuki,
The Rosser--Iwaniec sieve, unpublished notes, 14 March 2023, 102 pp.
https://www.suzuyu1729.com/notes/NagoyaSeminar2022/RosserIwaniec.pdf
Use: Proposition 9.4(i), pp. 22-23; Proposition 11.8, pp. 64-65;
Lemma 14.7, pp. 97-98. These are the dated notes cited in the current
positive-upper and Chen-lower modules (manuscript Appendices F-G).
