\subsection{Independent replay scope for the fifth-prefix subdivision}
\label{sec:independent-fifth-subdivision-replay}

The new replay source is
\texttt{independent\_fifth\_subdivision\_replay.py}. It imports neither
the author subdivision source nor any author or previous geometry helper.
Its companion JSON is the authoritative count, final status and hash
manifest. A partial status is not a complete replay. The mathematical
scope is numerical validation of the new within-parent subdivision
certificate under the already-reviewed Jensen/tree lemma. It is not an
independent validation of every analytic distribution interface.

\paragraph{Precisely what is inherited.}
The $292633$ parent coordinates and masks are bound by the original
witness SHA256. Ordered interiors, original first/third admission, all
full and selected square gates, and validity of the normalized density
are inherited from the previously reviewed tree and the locked full
independent Jensen replay. The old $30668$ Taylor replacements are
inherited only after checking each old NPZ against the hashes in its
locked independent replay, with each row matched to its parent.
The old Jensen rational floor is freshly recomputed and each old
whole-parent floor must match the new author's stored old floor.
These inherited claims are not silently recertified by the present run.

\paragraph{New geometry reconstructed independently.}
All $64$ children of each parent are rebuilt with widths $(2,2,2,2,1)$
on the grid $1600$. Four binary indices generate the first four offsets,
and the remaining two bits generate the four fifth-coordinate offsets.
This is a complete Cartesian partition in a different traversal order
from the author's. All $32$ vertices of each child are regenerated.

For an old selected subset, write $m_I=\sum_{j\in I}(2\ell_j+1)$
using its grid-$400$ parent. Its frozen affine upper plane is ascending
if $64m_I\le25\cdot400$, and descending otherwise. This independent
classification follows directly from the kink of $v(s)$ at $25/128$.
The constant alternative $153/256$ can never strictly beat both affine
planes at the midpoint; the equality tie chooses the ascending one.
Thus this classification reproduces precisely the parent's frozen
plane without using the author's three-entry minimum code.

The maximum of all fifteen planes and the old $W$ plane is recomputed
at every child vertex, as is the truncating plane $S_5+2t_5$. The
minimum of the selected new candidate's two affine planes is also
recomputed. Their vertex difference sum is consequently a freshly
calculated $d_\Sigma$, not a trusted witness field.

For either affine new plane, all coefficients of
$\text{plane}-S_5$ are negative. Fixing $t_5$, its ratio by $t_5$
is therefore maximal when the first four coordinates are at their
lower endpoints. The remaining affine-over-$t_5$ expression attains
its maximum at one of the two fifth endpoints. Taking the smaller of
the two plane maxima yields the independently reconstructed bound
$b=A/B$. This coordinate elimination is different from the author's
$32$-vertex ratio scan, while proving the same bound. For every
positive-mean child, the replay explicitly checks
\[
 2B<A\le4B,\qquad
 d_\Sigma B\le(A-2B)1024h_5.
\]
Thus $e=d_\Sigma/(1024h_5)$ lies in exactly the convexity interval
$0<e\le b-2$. Children of nonpositive mean receive zero.

\paragraph{An independent rational evaluator.}
The shared mathematical formula is necessarily
\[
 Q_b(e)=\frac{a\log((b-1)/(a-1))-e\log(a-1)}{ab},
 \qquad a=b-e.
\]
The implementation does not use the author's expanded integer
polynomial. Instead it builds the positive common-denominator atanh
series directly. If $L_m=\operatorname{lcm}(1,3,\ldots,2m-1)$, put
\[
 P_m(p,q)=\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}\frac{L_m}{2j+1}
                  p^{2j}q^{2m-2-2j}.
\]
Then the lower rational is
$2pP_m/(L_mq^{2m-1})$. The upper rational adds the positive geometric
tail $2(p/q)^{2m+1}/[(2m+1)(1-(p/q)^2)]$ by exact integer
common-denominator addition. The new replay uses four lower terms for
the positive logarithm and five upper terms for the subtracted one.
These are respectively at least and at most the author's three/four
term bounds. All rational products and floors use arbitrary-precision
integers; NumPy is used only for the independently rebuilt bounded
integer geometry. A separate direct \texttt{Fraction} construction
checks the rational engine on its explicitly enumerated test cases.
The startup test contains twenty interior cases. Five additional exact
$a=2$ cases, with $b\in\{9/4,5/2,3,7/2,4\}$, were checked without
changing the running source and are recorded in
\texttt{independent-fifth-subdivision-boundary-check.json}.

\paragraph{Every saved parent bound must be covered.}
The independently rounded child floors are summed across the complete
$64$-child partition. The resulting sum is compared by a maximum with
that parent's locked old bound. The replay asserts, separately for
every parent, that this independently valid maximum is at least its
saved new floor. Thus it cannot pass merely by matching a total sum,
nor by re-adding the old parent and selected children. Every changed
parent is covered by a newly recomputed child integral.
Checkpoint blocks save the independent parent floors, source and
target hashes, child count, domain slack and a stream digest of the
new child geometry and floors. Completed checkpoints are accepted
only under those bindings.

Python exact integer arithmetic, NumPy and the original coordinate
witness are common general-purpose foundations. The Jensen lemma and
the sieve density on $[2,4]$ are shared mathematical inputs. The new
implementation is an independent numerical replay using alternate
algebra and geometry; it is not a second independent proof of the
entire original analytic argument.

\paragraph{Completed result.}
The complete run finished normally, with status
\texttt{PASS\_INDEPENDENT\_FULL\_CHILD\_REPLAY}, after about $366$
seconds (execution session $9659$, now exited). It verified all
$292633$ parents and all $18728512$ children. Every saved parent
floor is covered. The minimum parent-floor slack is zero, as allowed
when the old certified bound is retained. Among positive-mean children
the minimum scaled integer convexity-domain slack is $8871936$.
Exactly $511336$ nonpositive-mean children receive zero, also agreeing
with the author count.

The stored certificate therefore passes this independent replay:
\[
 \Delta_5\ge
 \frac{3037921066812732539869}{302231454903657293676544}
 >\frac{10051}{1000000}.
\]
The stricter independent logarithm bounds yield a slightly larger
