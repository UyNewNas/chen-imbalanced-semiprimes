\paragraph{A cheap feedback of the new product constant into the two final costs.}
Put $K_0=67619/10000$, $K_1=33809/5000$, and
$C=70893/125000$.  The complete $n=48$ four-prime certificate already uses
$K_0$, rather than the older $6.7672$.  Its ordered cells $B$ partition
$\Omega_{10}$ and $\Omega_{11}$ as defined in
\texttt{four-prefix-safe-minimum.tex}.  Write
\[
 d\mu={dt_1dt_2dt_3dt_4\over t_1t_2t_3t_4},\quad
 Z_B=\int_B{d\mu\over t_3},\quad
 T_B=\int_B\omega\left({1-\sum_i t_i\over t_3}\right){d\mu\over t_3}.
\]
Here $t_3$ is the second smallest specified prime exponent.  On both
domains the Buchstab argument exceeds $(1-d-3h)/h>2$ and the main-scale
unit residual is absent.  The positive-product theorem supplies $K T_B$
as the switched upper coefficient for each fixed positive cell.  This
is an averaged cell statement, not an individual-prime-tuple assertion.

The saved witness records a method and a nonnegative rational saving
$g_B$.  For method zero its exact established meaning is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:four-min-k-fixed-switch-input}
 K_0T_B\le A_B:=K_0 C Z_B-g_B.
\end{equation}
Indeed a positive saved saving was rounded down from a lower enclosure
of $K_0 C Z_B$ minus an upper enclosure of $K_0\omega_B Z_B$.
If the saving is zero, $\omega\le C$ gives the same conclusion.
The old independent replay checks this particular saved method, rather
than merely checking some possibly different minimum.  No assertion of
the form \eqref{eq:four-min-k-fixed-switch-input} is made for a cell
whose saved method is an ordinary selected-subset or $W$ branch.

At the new constant, retain exactly the same switched choice.  Then
\[
 K_1T_B\le{K_1\over K_0}A_B
 =A_B-{K_0-K_1\over K_0}A_B.
\]
Consequently any rational lower bound $z_B\le Z_B$ gives the additional
cost reduction
\begin{equation}\label{eq:four-min-k-cheap-saving}
 d_B=\left[{K_0-K_1\over K_0}
                   (K_0 C z_B-g_B)\right]_+,
 \qquad {K_0-K_1\over K_0}={1\over67619}.
\end{equation}
Taking the positive part is legitimate because $A_B\ge0$, and retaining
the old bound is always an alternative.  This formula scales only a
proved switched cell bound.  It does not scale the entire $U_{10}$ or
$U_{11}$ cost, and it does not assume that the ordinary branch improves
when $K$ decreases.  Numerical slack in the old enclosures only weakens
the saved reduction; it does not invalidate this implication.

\paragraph{Purely rational mass, including repeated bins.}
Let the physical intervals be $[l_i/N,u_i/N]$, with $N=240000$.
The $U_{10}$ widths are all $343$; $U_{11}$ has first-coordinate width
$420$ and the other widths $343$.  For a consecutive group of $m$ equal
bins, retain its ordered simplex, and let $D_B$ be the product of these
$m!$.  Its Euclidean volume is the full rectangular volume divided by
$D_B$.  Positivity of the integrand therefore gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:four-min-k-rational-mass}
 Z_B\ge z_B:={N\prod_i(u_i-l_i)\over
                 D_B\,u_1u_2u_3^2u_4}.
\end{equation}
This remains true when the distinguished third slot lies inside a
repeated-bin block: only a constant pointwise lower bound is integrated,
not an incorrectly symmetrized $1/t_3$ moment.  All factors are rational;
no logarithm, Buchstab table, ordinary table, or new gate calculation is
needed.  Round \eqref{eq:four-min-k-cheap-saving} down at $Q=2^{80}$
and sum the resulting nonnegative integers.

\paragraph{Compatibility with the current parent replacement.}
The current cost also uses \texttt{four-min-priority40000-20261009}:
each selected parent retains the larger of its original saving and the
sum of its child's savings.  A parent feedback need not remain valid
on top of that maximum if an improved child uses the ordinary option.
Accordingly this certificate excludes all $40000$ such parents in their
entirety.  It also excludes every other cell with a nonzero method.
These exclusions leave disjoint, unchanged $n=48$ switched cells.  Their
new reductions may be subtracted from the current full cost once:
\[
 U^{\rm current}_{10}+U^{\rm current}_{11}
 \ \leadsto\ U^{\rm current}_{10}+U^{\rm current}_{11}
                    -\sum_{B\text{ retained}}d_B.
\]
The Chen coefficient gains one quarter of this sum.  All existing
ordinary/H-table choices, child replacements, and lower-start policies
stay unchanged.  The old $6.7672\to6.7619$ replacement and the old
$n=32$ logarithmic-mean experiment are not added again.

\paragraph{Scope and finite limits.}
The new program checks hashes, canonical old cell keys, the disjoint
parent exclusion, and every rational mass/floor in this formula.  It
does not rerun the old high-dimensional integrations; their saved
method-specific inequalities are explicit previously verified inputs.
The only new arithmetic input required for the analytic implication is
the same positive-product estimate with $K=K_1$ on these fixed positive
families.  As in the existing complete minimum proof, ordinary endpoint
realizations and null ordering walls use a common finite refinement
before $N$ tends to infinity.  The feedback uses no new cap or gate and
requires no uniform limit in a refining mesh.  A final rounded global
constant must reserve sufficient finite-compilation/asymptotic slack;
this local certificate itself does not change the project's main state.
