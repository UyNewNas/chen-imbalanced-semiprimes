Second implementation of the actual M1600 third-layer lower integrals
10 October 2026. All previous evidence and selected constants are unchanged.

Scope. The target is the two actually selected ideal I3 integrals at
h=19/125 and a=417/5000. The old M1600 records were new computations by
the already reviewed M800 engine, not a second-engine verification at
M1600. This program reconstructs their history cells and outer integral
without importing that engine or any of its geometry/numerical helpers.
It reuses only the independently reviewed160-bit interval/logarithm and
128-bit numerator-chord ordinary-sieve stream from the preceding round.

Geometry. Generate bins from1/M to each exact cutoff, including its last
partial bin. Start from all ordered bin pairs. A breadth-first history
partition retains a box only when x_upper+3y_upper<nu; it discards wholly
excluded boxes and unresolved depth-eight cells. An ordinary rectangle
is bisected in its longest coordinate. An equal-bin triangle is split
into two smaller triangles and their intervening rectangle. These child
regions are disjoint up to measure-zero faces. The complete retained cell
set is compared exactly with every row of the frozen witness, not just
its count. All unretained ideal contributions are nonnegative omissions.

The last variable r lies in(0,y_lower) on each core rectangle. Candidate
selected sums use masks1,2,3,7,5,6, together with the W minorant
153/256-3r/8. The middle plane(32-34s)/39 never controls the original
three-plane minimum: the other two intersect at s=25/128; below this
point (1+s)/2 is at most the middle plane, and above it57/64-3s/2 is at
most that plane. This is an exact global comparison, not a sampled one.

The new affine selector constructs a full rational wall arrangement.
For every plane and all four outer corners, it includes every zero of
the positivity condition V-nu and of the comparisons with its sibling
plane. It also includes every possible tie between center values. All
tests are affine in r, so their signs and the maximizing eligible plane
are constant on each open interval. A midpoint only selects the sign
on an already complete partition. Adjacent equal choices are merged.
This differs from the old per-plane feasible-interval construction.

The constant selector independently forms all corner-minimum affine
lines, enumerates every crossing and positivity root, takes each
candidate's minimum and then the maximum over candidates. Its active
pieces are merged. Both selectors are then intersected on a common
partition. Each piece receives the maximum of two valid lower bounds
for that same ideal integral; their sum is never used. Table-limit
omissions may differ between the two bounds and remain nonnegative.

Arithmetic and integration. The independent chord stream is stored so
the complete node arrays can be queried. Rational interpolation uses
the proved concavity/convexity direction and the h^2/8 error allowance.
All initial branches, delay indices and maximum argument32 remain
explicit. The reused stream is the independent oracle, not the previous
Q112 table being verified. The outer arithmetic and logarithms use160
bits with outward rounding. No floating-point number makes a decision.

For L(s)=s*fbar(s), the last-variable primitive is
 integral_left^right Fbar(d/r+b-1) dr/r^2
 = [L(d/left+b)-L(d/right+b)]/d.
Logarithmic product measures give exact mean and variance formulas.
The affine lower gap uses the same proved semiconvex Jensen inequality,
with an independently reconstructed curvature majorant. The only jump
of Fbar'' relevant to its monotone pieces is at3. Splitting at
d_min/(4-b),d_max/(4-b) avoids claiming global monotonicity; the
straddling band uses2 and the other bands use the explicit derivative
primitive. Mean rounding incurs its explicit integrated Lipschitz cost.
The constant method evaluates the common sum-corner gap and integrates
it with the same primitive. These mathematical inequalities are shared
stated inputs; a second program does not independently prove them.

Boundary pieces. An off-diagonal box also contributes its disjoint
r-in[y_lower,y] strip. A valid fixed cap is the minimum over all corners
for each candidate, then the maximum of these minima. Its conservative
gap at x_lower,y_lower,r=y_lower is multiplied by the exact strip mass
 4 log(x_upper/x_lower) [log(y_upper/y_lower)/y_lower
                         -1/y_lower+1/y_upper].
The old-minus-new ordinary gap decreases with its old argument because
Fbar is convex and decreasing. The common-sum and r endpoints used
here give the required lower bound. An equal-bin outer triangle uses
half of the full symmetric ideal core integral; the new numeric bound
is divided down by two. Its ordering strip remains omitted. Neither
strip nor diagonal totals may be added again after the final sum.

Certification boundary. A complete result evaluates every retained box
at both cutoffs and compares the full new rigorous lower sums with the
actually selected old lower sums. Individual new and old per-box floors
need not coincide. A pilot or a complete lower sum below the target is
labelled accordingly and cannot certify the old selection. The weighted
sum is I3(h)/4+3I3(a)/4, from two distinct terms of the original Chen
weight, and replaces the previous pair once. No new tightness is adopted
as gain and no K,c,b, manuscript or state is changed by this program.
