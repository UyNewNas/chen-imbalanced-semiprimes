3. Independent numerator-chord oracle

Let L(s)=s fbar(s), H(s)=s Fbar(s), with the usual ordinary normalization.
Inputs are the standard delay equations and their positivity/shape:
L=0 through2, H=1 through3,
L'(s)=H(s-1)/(s-1), H'(s)=L(s-1)/(s-1).
L is increasing concave above2, H increasing convex above3, and
|L''(u)|,|H''(u)|<=1/(u-1) on the pieces used below. Integer breaks are
mesh endpoints. Initial H=1 also gives L(s)=log(s-1) for2<=s<=4.

On a delayed interval [j/n,(j+1)/n], exact integration of a numerator's
linear endpoint chord divided byu has positive coefficients
 w0=(j+1)log(1+1/j)-1,
 w1=1-j log(1+1/j).
They have alternating series
 w0=sum_(k>=1)(-1)^(k+1)/(k(k+1)j^k),
 w1=sum_(k>=1)(-1)^(k+1)/((k+1)j^k).
The sixth partial sum is below and seventh above. For log(1+1/j) the
same even/odd rule applies. In common denominator840j^7, the lower
numerators are respectively
 420j^6-140j^5+70j^4-42j^3+28j^2-20j,
 420j^6-280j^5+210j^4-168j^3+140j^2-120j,
 840j^6-420j^5+280j^4-210j^3+168j^2-140j;
the upper numerators add15,105,120. The program evaluates integer Horner
polynomials and rounds these weights outward at2^-128. No logarithm oracle,
original table value or old interval object enters the independent update.

For a twice differentiable numerator V, chord error is bounded by
(M/2)(u-a)(b-u). After division byu>=a its integral is at most
M h^3/(12a). Taking M<=1/(a-1) gives1/[12 n j(j-n)].
Convex H is below its chord: subtract this error only from the lower L
increment. Concave L is above its chord: add it only to the upper H
increment. For target s<=4 use the explicit log increment instead.
All weights and numerator endpoints are nonnegative; each weighted sum is
then rounded outward again. Known monotonicity permits clipping a lower
update at its previous lower value. Delay indices are strictly earlier,
so induction proves every independently constructed node interval.

4. Exact streaming and target reproduction

Only the preceding node and nodes one unit earlier are needed. A ring of
n+2 entries for each of Llo,Lhi,Hlo,Hhi therefore retains every operand
until its final use. It begins with exact L=0,H=1 for indices<=2n. A write
at indexi cannot overwrite delayed indicesi-n-1,i-n or previousi-1,
whose index distances are all strictly smaller than n+2. This proves the
stream has exactly the same mathematical update as a full stored oracle.

To avoid multiple gigabytes of unneeded target arrays, the frozen target
recurrence is ALSO streamed. This transcription is openly the arithmetic
subject, not a source of independent bounds. Its trapezoid numerator
 [H(j-1)*j+H(j)*(j-1)]/[2j(j-1)] and the analogous L expression are
copied exactly, as are error_L=ceil(Q/(6n^3)),error_H=ceil(Q/(12n^3))
and max(previous lower,new lower). A direct source inspection establishes
the algebraic correspondence. Complete target instances at meshes15,64,129
are additionally constructed from the frozen class and all four components
of every updated node are required to match the stream exactly. The source
module is loaded ONLY for those target-transcription controls, never as an
oracle or a shared arithmetic primitive for the new enclosure.

A single oracle at n=262144,max32 is compared at aligned nodes to all
three actual target profiles. The initial [1,2] nodes coincide by definition;
every other target node is individually checked. Each target's lower bound
must be at most the oracle lower and its upper at least the oracle upper,

contains the actual ordinary functions. Successful complete output records

5. From all nodes to all actual queries and primitives

For any rational query in a grid cell entirely above the initial threshold,
concavity of L gives a lower endpoint chord and convexity of H an upper
endpoint chord. The opposite side is covered by h^2/8 since the curvature
bound is at most1 there. This is exactly the subject numerator() rule.
Delayed integer breaks are nodes; there is no use of a smooth remainder
across the initial jump. Endpoints are returned without interpolation.
Positive division bys gives the ordinary fbar/Fbar enclosure. The lower()
closed form on[2,4] is a separate already reviewed log routine; this report
does not mislabel it as a table lookup or re-run those logs.

The high-dimensional last-slice identities use J_F(s)=L(s+1),
J_f(s)=H(s+1), up to irrelevant constants, as follows by differentiating.
Their callers explicitly restrict s+1 to the table's maximum. Thus full
node containment plus the general interpolation proof applies to every
allowed query, not merely finitely sampled recorded arguments. It does
not re-prove the caller's rational clipping, sign, slice arrangement,
weighted-Jensen/corner geometry or sum; their original distinct reviews
remain necessary. No extrapolation beyond the stated maximum is licensed.
