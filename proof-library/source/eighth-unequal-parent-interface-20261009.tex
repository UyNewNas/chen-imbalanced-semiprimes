\subsection{An eighth continuation with unequal third and sixth envelopes}
\label{sec:eighth-unequal-parent-interface-20261009}

This note works from the actual upper tree selected in
\texttt{eighth-family-transfer-assembly-20261009.json}. In particular its
four nonsingleton sixth-prefix additions are already present. The note
does not certify a new integral or choose a new global constant.
Put $\mu=25/128$, $\Lambda=153/256$, $\eta=10^{-6}$, and
\[
 A(u)=(1+u)/2,\quad D(u)=57/64-3u/2,\quad v(u)=\min(A(u),D(u)),
 \quad S_j=\sum_{i\le j}t_i,\quad H_j=S_{j-1}+3t_j.
\]
Throughout $0<r=t_8<t_7<\cdots<t_1<\mu$. The actual third-level
activation condition $t_3>1/100$ is retained in the proposed cells.
Define the two different raw quantities
\begin{align*}
 L_3&=\max\{v(t_1),v(t_1+t_2),v(t_1+t_3),v(t_2+t_3),
                  v(S_3),\Lambda-3t_3/8\},\\
 R_6&=\max\{\Lambda-3t_3/8,\ v(s_I):\varnothing\ne I\subseteq[6]\}.
\end{align*}
In particular $L_3\le R_6$. No equality between them is presumed.

\paragraph{A third minorant is sufficient, and is not a new third cap.}
\label{par:eighth-unequal-minorant}
One may require only
\[
 H_3,H_5,H_7<B_3,\qquad B_3=v(t_2+t_3)\le L_3,
 \tag{U1}
\]
where its asserted ascending or descending formula is accompanied by
the corresponding active-side inequality. The other third branches
need not be at most $B_3$.

This is supported by the actual ordered-envelope identity, not by
treating every unlicensed raw tent as an available cap. In the main
source, Lemma \texttt{variable-envelope-simplification} proves that
the six-branch maximum equals the original gated eight-branch
expression for ordered triples below $\mu$. If a pair branch $v(s)$
can exceed $v(t_1)$, its original selected gate has surplus at least
$3/256$. If it cannot, the baseline $v(t_1)$ supplies the maximum.
The $W$ branch is likewise used with its baseline gate, rather than
with the gate removed. The robust coarse rule
\texttt{variable-prefix-coarse-cap} therefore approximates the
\emph{true} $L_3$ uniformly from below on compact ordered sets.
For $B_3=A(t_2+t_3)$ one can additionally check its particular pair
gate directly: $A(s)-(2t_1+s)\ge1/2-5\mu/2=3/256$ when $s\le\mu$.
The proof does not require this extra check when $B_3=D(s)$ is
dominated by another legal third branch.

Let $\mathcal C$ be a compact subset of all proposed strict domains.
The continuous quantity
\[
 d=\min_{t\in\mathcal C,\ j=3,5,7}(B_3-H_j)
\]
is positive. On the union of their third-prefix projections choose
the common old coarse rule so that
$L_3-d/3<\lambda_3\le L_3$. This choice is a function of the first
three labels only; it is not made separately for each eighth child.
Then $H_j<\lambda_3$ for all three histories. A common refinement
also preserves the old active labels and all earlier density
allowances. Boundary strips are paid from positive approximation
allowances. This is a statement about sufficiently accurate common
finite realizations of the endpoint policy, not about every old
coarse realization and not an assertion $\lambda_3=B_3$.

\paragraph{Actual sixth cap, rather than a hypothetical parent.}
Every selected sixth cap in the adopted tree is below its licensed
first-six subproduct tent, or inherits a fifth cap below the same
all-subset envelope. All adopted later changes are cap increases.
Consequently, on a retained path,
\[
 \lambda_3\le\lambda_6^{\rm cur}\le R_6.                 \tag{U2}
\]
This includes the latest nonsingleton sixth cells, even if they
overlap a proposed new eighth domain. Bounds obtained by changing
comparison envelopes, Jensen estimates or moment estimates change
the certification of a density, not the underlying cap. No obsolete
assertion $t_6<1/50\Rightarrow\lambda_6\le R_5$ is used.

The retained seventh odd cube follows from
$H_7<\lambda_3\le\lambda_6^{\rm cur}$. Thus the eighth node is
actually reached. Choose its new cap, after observing all eight
slots, so that
\[
 R_6<M<S_8+3r.                                         \tag{U3}
\]
The crucial lower bound is supplied by history, not by the upper
comparison level:
\[
 \lambda_6^{\rm cur}-S_8>H_7-S_8=2t_7-r>r.
\]
It follows that
\[
 1<\frac{\lambda_6^{\rm cur}-S_8}{r}
 \le\frac{R_6-S_8}{r}<\frac{M-S_8}{r}<3.               \tag{U4}
\]
The inequality $R_6-S_8>r$ alone would not imply the same assertion
for the actual inherited cap. Substituting it for the historical
argument would be a genuine gap; (U1)--(U2) avoid that substitution.

\paragraph{Support and exact sign.}
Let $I\subseteq[8]$ be a fixed selected mask, and list its complement
as $q_1<\cdots<q_m$. The initial conditions to be checked over each
whole label are
\[
 S_{j-1}+2t_j<M\ (1\le j\le8),\qquad
 s_I+\sum_{a<b}t_{q_a}+2t_{q_b}<M\ (1\le b\le m),
 \qquad M<v(s_I).                                    \tag{U5}
\]
The last inequality has a positive fixed license buffer. These are
the full-square and remaining-selected gates, not just a bound on
the product of the selected primes. Once all selected slots have
occurred, the remaining selected tests equal the residual full
square tests. For an ordinary upper residual, its admitted odd cubes
imply these squares at every later rank. Thus (U5) supplies the same
fixed-label factorization and endpoint slot-partition input as the
existing signed compiler; no new distribution exponent is required.

The even eighth node is retained. Attach an ordinary upper residual
at its new level. Its first exclusions have odd residual ranks, hence
odd full ranks $9,11,\ldots$. The upper pointwise property survives.
At a fixed finite policy the exact raw density difference is
\[
 V^{+}_{\rm cur}-V^{+}_{\rm next}
 =\sum_{P_8\in\mathcal S}g_N(P_8)
 \left\{V_N^+(N^{\lambda_6^{\rm cur}}/P_8,p_8)
              -V_N^+(N^M/P_8,p_8)\right\}.            \tag{U6}
\]
No coefficientwise monotonicity of finite signed weights is asserted.
The already established raw ordinary density equality, with
$\bar F(s)=1/s$ for $1<s\le3$, gives an endpoint saving at least
\[
 \boxed{\int_{\mathcal C}
 \frac{4(M-R_6)}{(R_6-S_8)(M-S_8)\prod_{i=1}^8t_i}\,dt.} \tag{U7}
\]
Replacing $\lambda_6^{\rm cur}$ by its larger majorant decreases
the difference of reciprocals, so the inequality points in the
required direction. The normalization is the same
$2e^{-\gamma}F=4\bar F$ as before. There is no Chen factor $1/4$
in this upper-$K$ saving and no factorial on the strictly ordered
physical domain.

\paragraph{Separation from old eighth nodes and from another raw winner.}
All thirty previously adopted eighth domains have the exact raw
identity $R_6=L_3$. A new domain must enforce $L_3<R_6$, rather than
merely name a different sixth winner. One sufficient finite affine
certificate is: select an upper plane for every old subset tent;
bound all 63 planes by the new winning $R_6$; require the winning
tent's active-side inequality; and make the first-three subset
upper planes and $\Lambda-3t_3/8$ strictly less than $R_6$.
These imply both the exact sixth envelope and the strict gap.
If these rows are proper affine constraints of a full-dimensional
polytope, they are strict on its interior; equality faces may be
trimmed. They must not be silently ignored if one is an identity.

In particular the proposed classes
\[
 R_6=A(t_1+t_4),\ B_3=D(t_2+t_3),\ I=\{2,4,8\},
 \quad\hbox{and}\quad
 R_6=A(t_2+t_5+t_6),\ B_3=A(t_2+t_3),\ I=\{4,5,6,8\}
\]
are disjoint in their strict winning interiors: a common point
would force $t_1+t_4=t_2+t_5+t_6$. This is a proper affine tie
wall. A deterministic priority rule or compact deletion handles
it; no two positive-volume contributions are counted there.
This does not claim exhaustion of all unequal-parent regions.

Sixth and eighth savings are additive by the ordered telescope
\[
 V_0-V_{\rm next}
 =(V_0-V_{\rm cur})+(V_{\rm cur}-V_{\rm next}).        \tag{U8}
\]
Hence an overlap with adopted sixth modifications is allowed.
They are already in $V_{\rm cur}$ and are not counted again. On the
new strict-gap domain no earlier eighth modification is active.
For a future action overlapping an existing eighth cap, (U6) must
instead compare with that actual eighth residual; (U7) cannot be
reused without checking its majorant.

\paragraph{Compactness, finite construction and numerical interface.}
Write $Q=S_7+4r$. From $H_3,H_5,H_7<L_3\le R_6<M<Q$ and order,
\[
 t_7<2r,\quad t_5,t_6<6r,\quad t_3,t_4<18r,
 \quad Q<2t_1+54r.
\]
Since $R_6\ge v(t_1)=(1+t_1)/2$ and $t_1<\mu$, necessarily
$r>53/13824$. Also $9r<H_7<\Lambda$ gives $r<17/256$.
All denominator factors in (U7) therefore remain positive and
bounded away from zero on the closure of the specified cells.
There is no new improper $r\to0$ tail. This does not remove the
separate $t_3>1/100$ activation condition of these witnesses.

Remove thin neighborhoods of proper order, history, license,
activation and winning walls. Freeze third caps first, then the
already adopted fourth/fifth/sixth rules on a common refinement,
and finally new eighth caps strictly between their inherited caps
and licenses and above all gates. Inactive new labels inherit the
old rule; an even node is never arbitrarily removed. Constant cap
identities retain their stipulated buffer. The original density
and compiler allowances are preserved; new positive allowances
pay the compact deletion, finite caps and compiler losses. Every
partition and buffer is fixed before $N\to\infty$. The same
signed-atom theorem may then be applied to each fixed admissible
outer prime-product family, with constants allowed to depend on
that finite construction. No uniformity in infinitely many labels
or interchange of a growing mesh with $N$ is used.

On a fixed affine cell the ten factors in (U7) are positive affine
functions and $g=M-R_6$ is affine and nonnegative. The reciprocal
product is convex. For an eight-simplex with nine vertices, put
$G=\sum_vg(v)>0$. Under density proportional to $g\,dt$,
\[
 \int g\,dt=\operatorname{vol}(T)G/9,\qquad
 E_g\ell=\frac{\sum_v\ell(v)+\sum_vg(v)\ell(v)/G}{10}.
\]
Thus its certified lower contribution is
$4\operatorname{vol}(T)(G/9)/\prod_jE_g\ell_j$.
Zero vertex gaps are harmless; $G=0$ gives zero contribution.
This supplies a numerical interface, not a replay of any cell.

\paragraph{Scope and source dependencies.}
This is a direct local analytic implication of the gated ordered
third-envelope lemma, ordinary raw-density equality and existing
finite signed compiler/distribution. Their local source locations
are recorded in the companion JSON. In particular it does not
reprove Lichtman's external distribution theorems, re-enumerate a
polytope, or verify a proposed cell's hypotheses from floating data.
No new ancestry, parity or same-tree obstacle is found for cells
satisfying (U1)--(U5) and the strict raw-parent separation above.
