\paragraph{Exact old branch separation.}

$a=2R-1=25/32-3d$. For each nonempty subset $I\subseteq[6]$,
\[
 v(s_I)\le R\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 s_I\le a\ \hbox{or}\ s_I\ge d,
 \qquad d-a=4(d-\mu)>0.
\]
Thus a complete assignment of the 63 subsets to the two closed halfspaces
specifies a convex rational polytope, and different assignments have
disjoint interiors. On its left branch the ascending plane is indeed
active, since $a<\mu$; on its right branch the descending plane is active,
since $d>\mu$. Merely bounding a tent by a chosen plane would already be
sufficient, but this stronger separation identifies the cells precisely.

All singletons are left: their sums are at most $t_1<\mu<d$, so the right
choice is unavailable. The winner subset $\{2,3\}$ is the identity $s_I=d$;
its zero row is retained as an identity, never required to be strict.
For ordered coordinates, prefix-count dominance of subsets implies
dominance of their sums, by summation by parts in the positive adjacent
coordinate gaps. Therefore left assignments propagate downwards and
right assignments upwards in this partial order. This finite reduction
was used for navigation only. No floating LP failure is promoted to an
exact infeasibility or completeness certificate.
