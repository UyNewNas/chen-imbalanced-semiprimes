0. Result, scope and author's role

This is not a numerical enumeration of a final sieve mesh, nor a new proof

1. What is fixed, and where the strict margin comes from

Fix a requested analytic error exponent A and the finite query graph:
the upper product policy, lower ordinary-start policies, all finitely many
marked-state queries used in the retained table generations, and the
positive-family instances used in the five selected costs. Fix k_star and
a_star>0 such that each original distributed prime is at least a fixed
constant times T^a_star and the number of these primes is at most k_star.
Minima are over THIS finite list; an arbitrary family whose cutoff tends
to zero with T is not covered. The choice of query graph precedes T.

Use the existing common finite-approximation lemmas to trim proper gate,
order and policy-switch walls and freeze coarse cap labels, within strictly
less than the allotted density error. Retain identity strata, inherited-cap
ties, and lower-dimensional unit pieces under their specified rules. They
cannot all be discarded as null sets. Choose outer smooth approximations
and a fixed positive radial smoothing tolerance at this stage too. Their
error allowances are measured in the original positive mass/normalization,
not relative to an arbitrarily small HB intersection.

Let mu>0 be a rational number below the minimum of the resulting finite
STRICT prefix-licence, W-initialization, active-history and physical-ceiling
reserves. A convenient definition is half the minimum of the infima of
these continuous gap functions on their retained compact pieces. Choose
coarse caps with their prescribed negative buffers before taking this
minimum. When a marked or classical state has a level1/2 endpoint, this is
an endpoint density limit; its finite implementation has a smaller level
and a positive reserve. The same applies at x=rho to v(x)=3x.

There is no assertion that the minimum over the raw CLOSED87 cells is
positive. Such a minimum is often zero. Nor is mu a number presently
stored for a completely materialized common global sieve mesh. Its positive
existence is supplied by the finite-policy approximation and robust coarse
licensing constructions, not by a PASS label on numerical data.

Ordinary residual-tail square inequalities are different. They follow from
the well support and the odd/even first-exit identity and may be weak.
They are not added to the strict-minimum list. At ordered upper endpoints
they cost O((J+2)tau) for rough rank at most J. Inflating the rough support
level by that amount restores them; the strict external licence absorbs
this inflation. Requiring a new positive margin for every residual square
would incorrectly create a circle between rough cutoff, rank and zeta.

The noncompact base third branch does not invalidate this construction.
extended-prefix-upper.tex, 'Margins at the larger first stop', fixes e,
chi and the finite cap list lambda_B=min(Theta-2e,Gamma_B-2e) BEFORE the
small-prime precision. It keeps t1<=rho-4e, giving a fixed first-cube
reserve. The robust envelope is used wholly below mu0-4e; the transition
uses the singleton fallback. Here mu0=25/128, rho=19/96; mu above denotes
the new licence reserve, not mu0. The tail bound in that source is uniform
in a sufficiently small range of these buffers. Thus decreasing the later
rough cutoff does not force the frozen coarse licence reserve to zero.

7. Smooth norms, beta truncation and arbitrary log precision

After sections1--4, the logarithmic-coordinate smooth functions satisfy
 D_T=sum_S ||partial_(2S) w_T||_1 <= C_rad (log T)^E,
with fixed E and C_rad. Original psi factors have uniform Euler derivative
bounds. The fixed radial/geometry smoothing tolerance affects C_rad and
the eventual threshold, not any exponent condition. It is not sent to
zero as a function of T. The number of length charts and HB terms is at
most (log T)^J0 times a fixed finite-policy coefficient norm.

Fix a desired recombined log exponent A_big, larger than A by all fixed
logarithmic costs of the finitely many queries. The divisor orders and
absolute tail moment exponent C_tail are fixed before any truncation.
Choose Fourier tail precision B>A_big+J0+C_tail+3, and truncate frequencies
at (log T)^(B+E+2). The Fourier L1 norm, not the number of individual
frequencies, is used for the retained separated integral. Request the
restricted convolution bound with extra exponent for this norm and for
chart recombination. Polynomial-log frequency variation in rough SW is
paid by a still stronger requested SW exponent.

For a complement bounded by tau(m)^B_beta, truncate only beta at
tau(m)<=(log x)^C. The deleted nonzero products have size
x(log x)^(E_tail-C), with E_tail independent of C, and the deleted exact
product T costs x^(4/5+o(1)) before any parity improvement. Choose C above
the required log exponent plus all fixed losses and E_tail+3. Normalize
the retained beta by (log x)^(B_beta C), and request P5.2 with an additional
B_beta C logarithmic precision. Alpha remains the original one-bounded
rough slot; no SW theorem for divisor-truncated alpha is required.

The auxiliary SW exponent on tau(d0) is fixed (one suffices here) and
does not grow with the requested log precision. Induced characters and
the Gram filter pay divisor moments of fixed order. The SW log exponent
may be increased last. Thus Fourier precision, beta threshold, source
precision and SW precision form a directed order, not an implicit fixed
point. Source polynomial-log norms and fixed constants are uniform in
the allowed target, original chart, chosen medium slot and endpoints.

8. Common threshold, errors, budgets and precise limitation

Once the preceding constants are fixed, choose T0 to satisfy ALL uniform
eventual inequalities, including the fixed chart comparisons, subpower
losses and classical input thresholds. The growing number of HB charts
does not permit taking a maximum of nonuniform thresholds per chart;
uniform norm/class bounds in sections4--7 are essential. Endpoints may
depend on T within this class, but coefficient templates, projector rule,
precision orders, cap policy and error allowances may not be chosen after
looking at progression errors.

Deleted low products contribute T^(1-kappa0+o(1)); rough collisions
contribute (T+T^theta_star)T^(-omega+o(1)); prime powers have the fixed
a_star saving; any exact complete product T is isolated, bounded by
T^(theta_star+o(1)). All are o(T/log^A T). Never evaluate tau(0).
No zero/parity shortcut is required for this parameter conclusion.

The present selected record has strictly positive upper/lower density and
compiler allowances, and strictly positive remaining upper/lower slacks.
Finite approximations in sections1 and3 may use fixed smaller fractions
of those allowances; asymptotic errors are then made smaller than the
remaining slack. The binding JSON checks positivity with exact Fractions.
This does not spend an existing allowance twice or change its value.
The positive singular series has a uniform positive lower bound, so an
absolute o(T/log^2 T) error is o(H_T). An arbitrary tiny HB mass is never
used as a denominator. Only already fixed marked families of positive
limiting mass are divided by that fixed mass in typed insertion.

Choose/prove the upper finite policy and its K theorem first. Lower queries
can then use that independent K in their finite positive-product costs.
Taking a common refinement for their finitely many queries does not use
the resulting c to prove K, and table iteration stays a finite induction.
There is therefore no feedback circle introduced by common parameters.

The weakest unresolved-by-this-document item is not a conflicting small
parameter inequality: it is the substantive finite-policy licensing and
density-approximation input which supplies the first compact realization
and mu. Its source constructions are identified above; it cannot be
replaced by a statement that all raw closed-cell inequalities are strict.
No new positive minimum for all untrimmed raw domains has been established
or claimed. Outside the fixed family, with shrinking cutoff or uncontrolled
smooth norms, this selection lemma gives no uniform theorem. Classical
spectral/packet, HB, sieve and number-theoretic estimates remain explicit
inputs. This report does not by itself certify the entire K,c,b theorem.
