counts of own-chen-table-insertion-chain-20261009.tex section1. For any
fixed subset E of pairs a<=u<h<=t<d, define R6(E) to be the Y6 terms
with (log_N p2,log_N p1)=(t,u) in E. Define R11(E) to be the full Y11
terms whose LAST TWO selected factors have exponent pair in E. Put

For squarefree rough n, a contributing Y11 tuple must have its first

otherwise a smaller unselected factor remains below the quotient cutoff.

and W_E<=W_full<=4*1_{Omega(n)<=2}. This is a pointwise argument and
does not use a lower bound on any negative count. Nonsquarefree rough
integers retain the same triangle bound1016 and O(N^(1-a)) exception,
since every new term is a subset of an old term with the same coefficient.
Unit and prime-divisor exceptions retain their old negligible treatment.
