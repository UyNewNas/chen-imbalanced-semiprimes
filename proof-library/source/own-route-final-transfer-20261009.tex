\subsection{The own-count route and its precise completion boundary}

For even $N$, use
\[
 \mathcal H_N=C_NN/\log^2N,\qquad
 C_N=\prod_{p>2}(1-(p-1)^{-2})
       \prod_{p\mid N,\ p>2}\frac{p-1}{p-2}.
\]
Let $D_{1,2}(N)$ count primes $p<N$ for which $N-p>1$ has at most
two prime factors counted with multiplicity.  For $0<b<1$, let
$D_b(N)$ count those with $N-p=rq$, $p,q$ prime, $r=1$ or prime,
and $r\le q^b$.  Count each $p$ once, using $r\le q$ for a
composite $N-p$.

\paragraph{The standard analytic inputs being used.}
The route uses the versioned L23 (arXiv:2309.08522v1) Proposition 5.2,
Proposition 3.2 and Lemma 3.4, the arbitrary-subinterval form proved
in Maynard II (arXiv:2006.07088v1), classical/generalized
Bombieri--Vinogradov strictly below $1/2$, ordinary prime and
M\"obius Siegel--Walfisz, the classical Rosser linear-sieve
first-exit density formulae and fundamental lemma, PNT/Mertens,
and the unconditional spectral bound $\sigma\le7/32$.
No EH or eigenvalue conjecture is added.  Accepting these cited
external theorems is different from leaving a new project interface
as an assumption.  The actual interfaces required below must be
proved by the named local constructions and checked against the
actual coefficients, rather than certified by status strings.

\paragraph{The bad-set subtraction is exact.}
Define
\[
 B_b(N)=\#\{p<N:N-p=rq,\ p,r,q\text{ prime},\ q^b<r\le q\}.
\]
Unique factorization gives the exact identity
$D_b(N)=D_{1,2}(N)-B_b(N)$ for the definitions above.  A prime
$N-p$ corresponds to $r=1$ and is always good.  A square $q^2$
is included in the bad set when $b<1$; it is not silently omitted
from this identity.  The diagonal may be discarded only as the
separately bounded $O(\sqrt N)$ error in the upper estimate.

For either value of $b$ used below, and more generally for
$1/2<b<1$, the common-weight upper theorem of
\texttt{actual-k-joint-theorem.tex}, with its density, compilation,
distribution and positive-assembly proofs, supplies
\[
 \limsup_{N\to\infty,\ 2\mid N}\frac{B_b(N)}{\mathcal H_N}
 \le K\log(1/b),\qquad K=\frac{67619}{10000}.
\]
Its finite upper strategy has strict density slack below $K$;
no single strategy is required to attain its limiting endpoint.
The moving-residue supremum and the positive radial smoothing in
that proof preserve every sufficiently large even target.  The
normalization $\log(1/b)$ comes from the ordered semiprime mass
$\int_{b/(1+b)}^{1/2}dt/[t(1-t)]$ and introduces no factor of two.

The exact rational logarithm enclosure then proves
\[
 c-K\log(50000/37433)>\frac1{100000},\qquad
 c-K\log(100000/74867)>\frac1{10000}.
\]
Subtracting limsup from liminf, and using these strict margins to
absorb the final errors, yields respectively
\[
 D_{37433/50000}(N)\ge\frac{\mathcal H_N}{100000},\qquad
 D_{74867/100000}(N)\ge\frac{\mathcal H_N}{10000}
\]
for every sufficiently large even $N$, provided the local own-count
interfaces just specified are established.  Since $C_N\ge C_2>0$,
both bounds imply existence for all such $N$.  No effective threshold
is claimed.

\paragraph{What still separates this synthesis from the full goal.}
This document supplies the final implication, not a replacement
for the local analytic and numerical proofs on which it depends.
The companion review must say whether those interfaces are actually
closed or remain assumptions.  A strict rational comparison proves
$0.74866<\exp(-1.9728/[2e^{-\gamma}6.06932])$, the one accepted
published-constant-derived benchmark previously used in the project.
It does not prove that this benchmark exhausts prior theorems or
all available parameter choices.  The current primary-source audit
and its unresolved full-text coverage are therefore kept separate.
Neither a smaller coefficient bound nor a collection of local
passes by itself establishes the requested superiority to every
frontier result.  Formalization and GitHub remain user-paused.
