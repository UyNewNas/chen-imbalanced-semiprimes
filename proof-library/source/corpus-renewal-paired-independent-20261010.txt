2. Derivation from the literal historical cell evaluator

On each E1 rectangle B=[tl,tr]x[ul,ur], put
 R=C-(5/2)t-u, C=57/64, a=417/5000,
 r=R(tr,ur), R0=R(tl,ul), sl=r/a, sh=R0/a,
 M=log(tr/tl)log(ur/ul),
 MR=C M-(5/2)(tr-tl)log(ur/ul)-(ur-ul)log(tr/tl).
The actual nonenhanced author function has exactly this active affine
residual plane. The other plane cannot win since t>=mu=25/128.
All E1 cells have2<sh<=9/4. For sl<2, all saved options are zero by the
source guards; there are227 such cells. Otherwise set
 f_l=log(sl-1)/sl, f_h=log(sh-1)/sh.
These are true ordinary fbar values on the explicit [2,3] branch, hence
at least the historical certified lower endpoint values used by the
original ordinary-function mesh. Both source options, in Chen units,
are bounded above respectively by
 C_B=f_l M/a,
 A_B=[f_l(R0 M-MR)+f_h(MR-r M)]/[a(R0-r)].
The two coefficients R0 M-MR and MR-r M are positive integrals; the
checker verifies strict positivity of their outward enclosures. Thus
substituting true endpoint values has the required direction even when
one of the original numerical endpoints was rounded further downward.
The max of the two original downward-rounded options is at most
max(C_B,A_B). A larger collection of overlapping ordinary estimates is
not being summed.

Let H_B be the minimum h248 entry over ALL closed table cells meeting
[sl,sh]. At a table knot both adjacent cells are included. The historical
lookup queries an outward enclosure, and hence may inspect more cells;
its minimum cannot exceed H_B. The old defect floor is therefore at most
H_B M/R0 in Chen units. The complete cell loss is bounded above by
 max(C_B,A_B)+H_B M/R0.

The independent code imports no original interval or sieve implementation.
It encloses these analytic upper bounds using decimal integer intervals
and alternating Mercator logarithms, with range reduction to[1,3/2].
The even partial sum and its next term provide both analytic endpoints;
all multiplications, divisions and cancellations are outward rounded.
This establishes the bound without independently reconstructing every
historical ordinary-function mesh point.
