\paragraph{Shared denominators in the full mixed-moment run.}
The full runner implements the preceding mixed-moment identity without
constructing seventy large intermediate rational numbers per simplex.
Let $L$ be a common denominator for all normalized factor and average
vertex values, and let $V_j=v_j/L$, $b_j(v_i)=b_{ji}/L$.
If $d_y$ is a common denominator for $Y(v_i)$ and $N_{jk}$ is the
integer coefficient in twice the weighted generating function, then
\[
 E_\nu[Y^k b_j^2]=\frac{k!N_{jk}}{Gd_y^kL^2(10)_{k+2}}.
\]
Using
$\binom{9+k}{k}k!/(10)_{k+2}=1/((k+10)(k+11))$ gives the $k$th
contribution to the correction directly as
\[
 Q^{(k)}=\frac{U^{-10}}{2Gd_y^k(k+10)(k+11)}
                       \sum_j\frac{N_{jk}}{v_j^2}.
\]
A least common multiple of the $v_j^2$ evaluates the inner sum with
integers. Each individual mixed-moment floor is nevertheless retained
in the witness by direct integer division, including every factor and
degree. This is algebraically the same author computation as the frozen
pilot; every one of the128 pilot rows is compared during the full run.
The optimized runner is not called independent verification.
