\subsection{A tent34 upgrade of the actual current sixth node}
\label{sec:sixth-tent34-upgrade-analytic}

The comparison in this note is against the adopted thirteen-cell
tree, including its descending33 rule. It is not a second estimate
against the fifth tree. The source of the exact signed identity is
\texttt{actual-k-compilation-joint-audit.tex}, paragraphs1 and6;
the residual-density normalization is that in the sixth-saving and
telescoping sections of \texttt{actual-k-density-joint-audit.tex}.
The present note checks
the changed actual old cap and the resulting same-node telescoping.
It does not enumerate or integrate the proposed new cells.

\paragraph{Domain and explicit new support conditions.}
Put $(x,y,z,w,p,r)=(t_1,\ldots,t_6)$ in strictly decreasing order,
$x<\mu=25/128$, $r<1/50$, and $S_j=\sum_{i\le j}t_i$.
Let
\[
 A(s)=(1+s)/2,\quad D(s)=57/64-3s/2,\quad
 v(s)=\min\{A(s),D(s)\},\quad \eta=10^{-6}.
\]
Require the actual third endpoint and the complete old fifth
envelope to be
\[
 L_3=R_5=R_0=A(x),\qquad
 R_5=\max\{W,v(s_I):\varnothing\ne I\subseteq[5]\},
 \quad W=153/256-3z/8.
\]
Thus all31 old subset values and $W$ are bounded by $R_0$;
merely naming $R_0$ without these conditions would be insufficient.
Retain the old histories
\[
 H_3=x+y+3z<R_0,\qquad H_5=S_4+3p<R_0,
\]
implemented below the same actual buffered third cap after common
finite refinement. Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tent34-new-and-comparison-caps}
 C=S_5+4r-\eta,\quad A_{33}=A(x+r),\quad D_{33}=D(x+r),
 \quad R=\max\{R_0,D_{33}\},\quad M=v(y+r)-\eta.
\end{equation}
The new strict selectors are $C>A_{33}$ and $M>R$.
Use selected mask34 $=\{2,6\}$, retain all complete squares
$S_{j-1}+2t_j<M$ for $1\le j\le6$, and all four remaining-selected
gates
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tent34-selected-gates}
 \begin{split}
 y+r+2x&<M,\\
 x+y+r+2z&<M,\\
 x+y+z+r+2w&<M,\\
 S_5+p+r&<M.
 \end{split}
\end{equation}
Both selected tent licenses exceed $M$ by at least $\eta$.
Checking only the first gate would not supply the full residual
factorization interface; all four are part of the hypothesis.

\paragraph{Exhausting the old sixth rules on this domain.}
The old clipped family is inactive. To see this for every selected
mask, not only the three formerly retained masks, the full fifth
envelope gives, for each nonempty old subset sum $B$,
\[
 B\le x\quad\hbox{or}\quad B\ge b(x)=25/96-x/3>\mu.
\]
Masks omitting slot six have license at most $R_0$; singleton six
does not improve $R_0$. For a remaining mask $B\cup\{6\}$, the
second alternative gives $v(B+r)<v(B)\le R_0$. The first gives
$v(B+r)\le A(B+r)\le A_{33}<C$. No such mask licenses the adopted
clipped cap $C$ here. This statement refers to that fixed earlier
policy, not to every imaginable cap lower than $C$.

The old ascending33 rule is also inactive, since
\[
 D_{33}<M\le A(y+r)-\eta<A_{33}-\eta.
\]
It required $A_{33}-\eta<D_{33}$, the opposite strict inequality.
The original saved-sixth cover has $r\ge1/50$; the above-$\mu$
fallbacks have $x>\mu$; the pair17/pair12 expanded-envelope cells
have $R_5>R_0$. All are excluded. The moment improvement on the
old eleven cells altered no policy or cap.

Consequently the actual current sixth cap is either the actual
inherited fifth cap $\lambda_5$, or a licensed frozen version of
the old descending33 endpoint $D_{33}-\eta$. Denote it by
$\lambda_{6,\mathrm{old}}$. The crucial enclosure is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tent34-actual-old-sixth-bound}
 \lambda_5\le\lambda_{6,\mathrm{old}}
 \le\max\{R_0,D_{33}-\eta\}\le R<M.
\end{equation}
The upper envelope $R$ is deliberately conservative. It need not be
an available old cap, and equality with the old actual cap is not
asserted. In particular the proof must not continue to substitute
$\lambda_5$ for the actual old sixth cap where descending33 is active.

\paragraph{Residual ranges and a fixed positive cutoff.}
The new range condition follows already from the selectors:
\[
 M\le A(y+r)-\eta<A_{33}-\eta<C-\eta
       =S_5+4r-2\eta,
 \qquad M-S_6<3r-2\eta<3r.
\]
Also $H_5-(S_6+r)=2(p-r)>0$. Preserving $H_5$ below the actual
third cap makes the actual inherited fifth argument exceed one.
Since all earlier sixth actions were upgrades, the complete chain is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tent34-residual-range}
 1<\frac{\lambda_5-S_6}{r}
 \le\frac{\lambda_{6,\mathrm{old}}-S_6}{r}
 \le\frac{R-S_6}{r}<\frac{M-S_6}{r}<3.
\end{equation}
The weaker endpoint condition $R_0-S_6>r$ alone would not justify
the actual first inequality without this history-preserving
ancestor approximation.

Because $H_3,H_5<R_0<C$, one has
$p<2r-\eta/2$, $z,w<6r$, and
$R_0<C<x+y+18r$. Hence
\[
 r>r_*:=\frac{1-3\mu}{36}=\frac{53}{4608}>1/100.
\]
The already used third cutoff $1/100$ therefore suffices. All
factors in the proposed density remain positive on compact closures;
in particular $R-S_6\ge R_0-S_6\ge r\ge r_*$ there. This is a
bound for the stated comparison-envelope domain, not for all
possible improvements merely satisfying $M>\lambda_{6,\mathrm{old}}$.

\paragraph{Same-node replacement and exact signs.}
Retain membership of every prefix through rank six. At that node
replace its old residual upper tree by the one at the larger
licensed cap $M$, using only information in the six known slots.
The residual type remains $D^+$, so subsequent first exclusions
are at residual odd ranks and therefore at full odd ranks. No new
even exclusion is made. The complete and selected gates above,
followed by the ordinary residual well support, are exactly the
support conditions propagated in the existing compiler proof.

For a fixed retained prime prefix $P_6$, the raw old-minus-new
signed subtree identity is
\[
 g_N(P_6)\left(V^+_{N^{\lambda_{6,\mathrm{old}}}/P_6}
                  -V^+_{N^M/P_6}\right),
\]
with the same residual prime universe below the last prefix prime.
The source-checked ordinary raw-density equality
$\bar F(s)=1/s$ on $1<s\le3$ applies to both actual subtrees by
\eqref{eq:tent34-residual-range}. Their limiting normalized saving is
\[
 4\left(\frac1{\lambda_{6,\mathrm{old}}-S_6}
                -\frac1{M-S_6}\right)
                 \frac{dt_1\cdots dt_6}{\prod_i t_i}.
\]
It decreases as the old cap increases, with derivative
$-4/(\lambda_{6,\mathrm{old}}-S_6)^2$. Thus a valid conservative
increment is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tent34-upgrade-density}
 k_{34}(t)=\frac{4(M-R)}{(R-S_6)(M-S_6)\prod_i t_i}.
\end{equation}
No finite pointwise monotonicity of signed weights, or subtraction
of two unrelated one-sided density bounds, is used.

Let $T_5$ be the common fifth tree, $T_{13}$ the adopted current
upper tree and $T_{34}$ the tree after this replacement. On an
overlap with the old descending33 action the exact density identity
is
\[
 V(T_5)-V(T_{34})=
 [V(T_5)-V(T_{13})]+[V(T_{13})-V(T_{34})].
\]
The old descending gain remains the first summand; only
\eqref{eq:tent34-upgrade-density} lower-bounds the second. Adding
a fresh $\lambda_5\to M$ gain instead would double count the
intermediate improvement. Disjointness from the old descending
domain is unnecessary; disjointness within the new same-node
certificate cover remains necessary.

\paragraph{Four affine charts and boundary handling.}
Split according to $R_0>D_{33}$ or $D_{33}>R_0$, and independently
according to $A(y+r)<D(y+r)$ or its reverse. The latter split is
equivalently $y+r<\mu$ or $y+r>\mu$. On each chart $R$ and $M$
are affine, so the gap $M-R$ and all eight denominator factors
in \eqref{eq:tent34-upgrade-density} are affine. Weighted Jensen
and its finite log-moment refinement apply exactly as previously,
with the physical volume and no new ordering factorial.

The two comparison branches agree on $R_0=D_{33}$ and the two
new cap branches agree on $y+r=\mu$. Thus there is no density jump
at either tie. For a finite proof one may trim these proper affine
walls and retain the old rule there, or assign them once by a fixed
half-open convention. Do not count closed-cell intersections twice.
Constant identities such as the active license minus the proposed
cap being exactly $\eta$ remain valid and are not trimmed.
The old subset-label partition and old sixth activation boundaries
must also be retained in the common refinement, even though the
conservative formula itself no longer depends on which old action won.

\paragraph{Finite common realization and retained budgets.}
Start with one common construction of the current thirteen-cell
endpoint and all of its already allowed errors. On compact subsets
of the new domain preserve the old projected third/fourth/fifth
labels, actual sixth activation labels, and all ancestor history
margins. The old sixth cap can be kept below its pointwise endpoint,
and hence below $R$. On sufficiently small sixth pieces choose one
new cap below the whole-piece infimum of $M$, above the whole-piece
supremum of $R$ and all new gates, with a further fixed positive
buffer. The strict gap and uniform continuity make this possible.
The new ordinary range is preserved because the frozen new cap is
at most the pointwise $M$. No earlier node is allowed to inspect
later prime slots.

Outside these new compact pieces leave the current thirteen-cell
rule unchanged. This is a common refinement of the actual current
raw tree, not a demand that every previously chosen coarse finite
implementation already support the upgrade. The bounded density
and the fixed positive lower cutoff allow the new approximation
loss to tend to zero while meeting all old positive allowances.
Compiler errors are handled for this one finite resulting policy,
with levels, masks, labels and smoothing fixed before $N\to\infty$.
Its signed distribution and positive-product interfaces have the
same hypotheses as before. The comparison function $R$ itself
does not have to satisfy a selected-product license.

If a later independent integral certificate gives an additional
saving $D_{34}>0$, subtract only $D_{34}$ from the current raw
endpoint and retain every accumulated upper budget. Any new
approximation and compilation allowances must be paid separately.
Neither the old thirteen-domain gains nor any lower Chen feedback
is needed to justify this local upper-tree replacement. No value
of $D_{34}$, $K$, $c$ or $b$ is selected by this analytic note.
