\paragraph{A distinct quadrature and derivative implementation.}
No author interval, logarithm, automatic derivative, quadrature or
clipping routine is imported.  Arithmetic uses directed integers with
denominator $2^{112}$.  Logarithms are reduced to $2\operatorname{atanh}z$
with $0\le z\le1/3$; 64 positive terms are evaluated with outward
rounding and the exact geometric remainder
\[
 0\le 2\operatorname{atanh}z-
 2\sum_{j=0}^{63}\frac{z^{2j+1}}{2j+1}
 \le\frac{2z^{129}}{129(1-z^2)}.
\]
Powers of two are accounted for with the corresponding outward
enclosure of $\log2$, including reversal of endpoints for a negative
integer multiple.  All denominators and logarithm arguments are
checked positive.

Every smooth integrand needed here has the form $L(u)T(u)$, where
\[
 L(u)=\sum_i c_i\log(A_i+B_i u),\qquad c_i\in\mathbb Z,
 \quad T(u)=\frac1u\quad\hbox{or}\quad
 T(u)=\frac1{u(1-u)}=\frac1u+\frac1{1-u}.
\]
The intervals lie inside the positivity domain of every affine factor.
For $1\le k\le4$ the independent implementation uses explicitly
\[
 L^{(k)}(u)=(-1)^{k-1}(k-1)!
 \sum_i c_i\left(\frac{B_i}{A_i+B_i u}\right)^k,
\]
and
\[
 T^{(k)}(u)=\frac{(-1)^k k!}{u^{k+1}}
 \quad\hbox{or}\quad
 \frac{(-1)^k k!}{u^{k+1}}+\frac{k!}{(1-u)^{k+1}}.
\]
Leibniz's formula $ (LT)^{(4)}=\sum_{k=0}^4
\binom4kL^{(k)}T^{(4-k)}$ supplies an interval enclosure on each
whole panel.  This is not a reimplementation of the author's second-
derivative jet arithmetic and does not differentiate an interpolant.
