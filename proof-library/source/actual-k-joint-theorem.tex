\subsection{The common-weight upper theorem and its two Chen corollaries}
\label{sec:actual-k-joint-theorem}

Write
\[
 C_N=\prod_{p>2}\left(1-\frac1{(p-1)^2}\right)
          \prod_{p\mid N,\ p>2}\frac{p-1}{p-2},\qquad
 \mathcal H_N=C_N\frac{N}{\log^2N}.
\]
The analytic inputs in this section are the versioned L23
(arXiv:2309.08522v1) Propositions 5.2 and 3.2 and Lemma 3.4,
the arbitrary-subinterval form in Maynard II
(arXiv:2006.07088v1), classical/generalized Bombieri--Vinogradov
strictly below $1/2$, ordinary Siegel--Walfisz, the fundamental
lemma and ordinary upper/lower linear-sieve density equalities,
PNT and the unconditional spectral bound $\sigma\le7/32$.
Their exact uses and primary-source versions are specified in the
joint distribution audit. No Elliott--Halberstam or Selberg
eigenvalue conjecture is imposed. These external theorems are used
as mathematical inputs; their full proofs are not reproduced here.

\paragraph{Derived upper theorem.}
For every fixed $1/2<b<1$, define
\[
 B_b(N)=\#\{p<N:p\text{ prime},\ N-p=rq,\quad
                    q^b<r\le q,\ r,q\text{ prime}\}.
\]
The fourth/fifth/sixth finite-tree construction, with its bound
certificates and the interface proofs listed below, gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:actual-k-joint-upper-theorem}
 \limsup_{\substack{N\to\infty\\2\mid N}}
       \frac{B_b(N)}{\mathcal H_N}
 \le\frac{67619}{10000}\log(1/b).
\end{equation}
The error is uniform along even $N$. The factor cutoff and every
strategy and approximation parameter are fixed before this limit.

\paragraph{Proof by a common finite object.}
The numerical upper bound for the endpoint density is
\[
 E=\frac{2444205458396424207668787105}
         {361468820064774123237146624}
 < K:=\frac{67619}{10000}.
\]
Fix $E<K_0<K$; for example $K_0=(E+K)/2$.
The proof in \texttt{actual-k-density-joint-audit.tex} starts from
one extended third-prefix tree and successively makes the fourth,
fifth and sixth modifications. Its identities telescope between
these same successive trees. The old fourth and sixth witnesses
are replaced; only the fifth coverage outside the old fifth
parents is added. The exact rational upper bound is
\[
 U_0-D_4-D_{5,O}-D_{5,\mathrm{new}}-D_6=E.
\]
The strict difference $K_0-E$ pays for all endpoint, buffer,
coarse-history, fine-box and small-prime errors. The density proof
therefore supplies one legal finite buffered strategy, with fixed
history and positive margins, whose compiled density is below
$K_0 C_N/\log N$ asymptotically. It does not require a finite
strategy to attain $E$ exactly.

Apply \texttt{actual-k-compilation-joint-audit.tex} to that strategy.
Odd first exits give the pointwise upper property. Coefficientwise
even-outer/odd-inner boxing, followed by sign-sensitive attachment
of $\beta^\pm$, produces a single coefficient sequence $\rho_N$,
$|\rho_N(d)|\le1$, with this same density bound. The exact labelled
convolution identity retains each repeated-label factorial once.
All selected full and remaining-square gates propagate to every
descendant. Endpoint greedy factorization supplies a fixed slot
partition on each box for every required Type II scale, including
the two-capacity case requiring full reassignment. The linked
parameter order $\eta\mapsto(\ell,\omega)\mapsto M\mapsto\tau$
provides the small-factor and fine-box losses without a circular
choice. Thus this very $\rho_N$, rather than an arbitrary weight
on its support, has the exact atom data required next.

Use \texttt{actual-k-distribution-joint-audit.tex} with those data.
The individual rough HB slot satisfies full auxiliary SW by
\texttt{focused-rough-sw-product-interface-audit.tex}.
The other HB alternatives have at most two original groups and
are covered by the explicitly checked one/two-long estimates.
The factorization margins imply all four L23 size inequalities;
the moving residue $N$ is in the source supremum on the retained
local scales. Low ranks stay strictly below $1/2$. Divisor-weighted
square collisions cost $(N+D)N^{o(1)}/N^\omega$, which is a power
saving at these fixed parameters. These steps prove signed
distribution with the exact coprime mass for the same $\rho_N$.
They make no alteration to its density constant.

Finally \texttt{actual-k-positive-assembly.tex} removes the exact
coprime mass after summing the moduli, with error
$O(X_WN^{-a}\log N)$. It applies the pointwise sieve majorant to
a nonnegative smooth outer approximation before using signed
distribution. Low products, small prime values of $N-rq$, squares
and the value $rq=N$ are all treated explicitly. In exponent/radial
coordinates $(t,y)$, the bad-family mass is
\[
 \int_0^1dy\int_{b/(1+b)}^{1/2}\frac{dt}{t(1-t)}=\log(1/b).
\]
Take $N\to\infty$ for each fixed approximation and then remove
the outer smoothing. This gives the stronger intermediate upper
bound $K_0\log(1/b)$, hence
\eqref{eq:actual-k-joint-upper-theorem}. No factor of two is
introduced, because $r\le q$ counts each composite once.

\paragraph{The lower-count input remains logically separate.}
Let $D_b(N)$ count the desired representations with $r=1$ or prime
and $r\le q^b$. For any established bound
$D_{1,2}(N)\ge(c-o(1))\mathcal H_N$, subtracting the bad set gives
\[
 \liminf_{\substack{N\to\infty\\2\mid N}}
 \frac{D_b(N)}{\mathcal H_N}\ge c-K\log(1/b).
\]
Using Wu's 2008 theorem ($c=899/1000$) yields
$D_{2189/2500}(N)\ge\mathcal H_N/2000$ eventually.
Using Li's published 2026 Theorem 1.1 ($c=1233/625$) yields
$D_{747/1000}(N)\ge\mathcal H_N/2500$ eventually.
Both normalizations, unit conventions and strict rational margins
are checked in the separate source/transfer fragments and
\texttt{certify\_actual\_k\_assembly.json}.
No $H/h$ table improvement is added to either published constant.

The latter is an unconditional corollary \emph{when the cited
unconditional Li theorem is taken as an external input}. This
does not claim that the present work has independently repaired
the two proof-interface questions recorded for Li's derivation.
The Wu corollary avoids that input but is not a substitute for
the stronger target: $0.8756$ exceeds the separate recorded
published-method benchmark $0.749$. By contrast $0.7470$ is below
both that benchmark and the direct Li--Liu $0.9/0.894$ statements.
