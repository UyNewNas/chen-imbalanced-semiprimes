\paragraph{A weighted generating function for exact mixed moments.}
Let the nine vertex gap values be scaled to $g_i\ge0$, with $G=\sum_i g_i>0$.
The probability measure is the mixture of Dirichlet parameters
$(1,\ldots,1)+e_i$ with probabilities $g_i/G$, of total parameter10.
For rational vertex values $y_i,b_i$, set
\[
 H(t)=\prod_{i=1}^9(1-ty_i)^{-1},\quad
 B_1(t)=\sum_i\frac{b_i}{1-ty_i},\quad
 B_2(t)=\sum_i\frac{b_i^2}{(1-ty_i)^2},
\]
\[
 W_0(t)=\sum_i\frac{g_i}{1-ty_i},\quad
 W_1(t)=\sum_i\frac{g_i b_i}{(1-ty_i)^2},\quad
 W_2(t)=\sum_i\frac{g_i b_i^2}{(1-ty_i)^3}.
\]
The mixture generating function is
\[
 F(t,u)=\prod_i(1-ty_i-ub_i)^{-1}
                  \sum_i\frac{g_i}{1-ty_i-ub_i}.
\]
Twice its coefficient of $u^2$ equals
\[
 H(t)\bigl(2W_2+2B_1W_1+(B_1^2+B_2)W_0\bigr).
\]
Consequently
\[
 E_\nu[Y^k b^2]=\frac{k!}{G(10)_{k+2}}
 [t^k]H(t)\bigl(2W_2+2B_1W_1+(B_1^2+B_2)W_0\bigr).
 \tag{M}
\]

It follows directly from the Dirichlet multinomial moment formula;
the square ensures nonnegativity of the final expectation, although
individual intermediate coefficients can have either sign.
