\subsection{Independent review of the replacement fourth-prefix certificate}
\label{sec:stronger-fourth-independent-review}

The scope of this review is the local functional in
\texttt{fourth-prefix-stronger-refinement.tex}, the frozen finite rectangle
witness, and its exact lower bound. The previously derived distribution,
factorization, and ordinary density identities remain inherited hypotheses.
This review does not certify the complete representation theorem.

\paragraph{Correlated bounds and the averaged gap.}
Write $L_j=\min_{b\in\mathcal B_j}b$, $L=\max_jL_j$, and
$M=\min_{a\in\mathcal A}a$. Pointwise,
\[
 M-L=\min_{j,a}\max_{b\in\mathcal B_j}(a-b).
\]
For each fixed $j,a$, every affine difference is at least its box infimum.
Taking its maximum over $b$, then the minimum over $j,a$, proves the
claimed constant gap lower bound. No interchange of an infimum and a
maximum as an equality is used.

Choose one affine upper piece $b_j$ of each old component and put
$U=\max_jb_j$. Then $U\ge L$, and $G=M-U$ is concave. At any point of a
rectangle, its multilinear corner weights are nonnegative, sum to one,
and express that point as a convex combination of the sixteen vertices.
Consequently $G(t)\ge\sum_v\lambda_v(t)G(v)$. Since
$\int_B\lambda_v=|B|/16$, it follows that
\[
 \int_B(M-L)\ge\int_BG\ge\frac{|B|}{16}\sum_vG(v).
\]
The maximum of this average bound and the constant bound is a valid
integral lower bound. A strictly positive constant gap is checked
separately; the average is never used to license a pointwise failed gate.
The implementation's rounded integer center merely chooses the pieces
$b_j$; it is not needed to be the geometric center for this argument.

\paragraph{Derivative envelope.}
The stated ordinary sieve equations give
\[
 \mathcal N(s)=A(s)-\frac{s}{s-1}\log(s-2),\qquad
 \mathcal N'(s)=\frac{s}{(s-1)^2}
 \left(\log(s-2)-\frac{s-1}{s-2}\right).
\]
The factor $s/(s-1)$ is present. For $1\le u\le3$,
$1+1/u-\log u$ is decreasing and positive at three. The latter follows
from $\log3<10/9<4/3$, for example by the positive atanh series with
$z=1/2$. Thus $\mathcal N$ decreases on $[3,5]$; it equals one on
$(1,3]$. The logarithmic integrand for $A$ is increasing, so left
Darboux sums are lower bounds. Upper rounding of the subtracted logarithm
term is also in the required direction.

The independent replay uses forty-eight logarithmic terms and a
$2^{112}$ scale. Its bounds, rounded down to the $2^{80}$ scale,
dominate every one of the author's $8193$ stored lower entries, which
were obtained with twenty-four terms. All stored entries are positive.
This independently validates the entire table, including the endpoint
at five. Upward rounding of a real argument to the next grid point is
valid by the proved monotonicity of $\mathcal N$, even if the stored
rounded entries themselves are not assumed monotone.

\paragraph{Residual and integration directions.}
For each component and each point,
$\min_b(b-S)\le\min_b\sup_B(b-S)$. Taking a maximum over components
proves the proposed $R_0$ upper bound. The analogous bound for $M-S$,
enlarged by $R_0$, gives $R_1\ge R_0$. At every point,
\[
 0<r_\theta=(1-\theta)(L-S)+\theta(M-S)
 \le R_\theta=(1-\theta)R_0+\theta R_1.
\]
The old third cube ensures $r_0/w>1$, and the checked bound
$R_1/\ell_w<5$ keeps all arguments in the positive table range.
On the $j$th quarter interval, monotonicity and positivity give
\[
 \frac{\mathcal N(r_\theta/w)}{r_\theta^2}
 \ge\frac{c_j}{R_\theta^2},\qquad
 \int_{(j-1)/4}^{j/4}\frac{d\theta}{R_\theta^2}
 =\frac1{4R_{(j-1)/4}R_{j/4}}.
\]
The identity also holds when $R_0=R_1$. The substitution from sieve
argument to residual level contributes one factor $w$, canceling one
of the two fourth-coordinate factors in the original kernel. With
$r=DR_0$, $q=DR_1$, and $d=D\widehat d_B$, this gives precisely the
integer contribution coefficient $16D d$ stated in the certificate.
All extracted factors are uniform lower bounds multiplying a positive
pointwise gap, so use of the averaged gap is justified.

\paragraph{Geometry, replacement, and frozen caps.}
The replay uses explicit evaluation at all sixteen vertices to compute
affine extrema, rather than the author's coefficient-sign implementation.
It checks the original third cube, all four complete square gates,
every selected-remaining gate, strict cap improvement, ordered boxes,
and the upper argument bound. Subsets not containing the fourth slot
are already old branches or dominated singleton branches on the stated
$x<39/200<25/128$ domain.

Dyadic rectangles are checked inside their individual $1/200$ root
cubes, then separated recursively along independently chosen midpoint
cuts. Boundaries may meet, but interiors cannot overlap. The integrals
are summed only over those interiors. This is a complete replacement
of both old fourth-prefix contributions; neither old gain is added.

The original numerical rectangle need not satisfy
$\inf_B M>\sup_B L$. Hence it cannot automatically receive a single
constant level. The final source correctly requires further finite
common refinement of the old third labels and new fourth labels.
Uniform continuity and the checked pointwise positive margins permit
legal frozen levels on the refined labels, with an arbitrarily small
loss in the total integral. The strict gap above the rounded final
gain absorbs that loss and the boundary trimming. A later fifth-prefix
certificate whose old cap bound covers every eligible four-slot cap
continues to cover this replacement tree.
