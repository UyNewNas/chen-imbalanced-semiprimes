Two precise interface qualifications to the current lower assembly
2026-10-09. Supplement to the frozen lower-density assembly; no old file changed.

This note supersedes only the unnecessarily strong relative-error wording
of section 0 and the extracted W1 integral notation of section 4 in
lichtman-current-lower-density-assembly-20261009.txt. Its scalar ledger,
table occurrences, conditional scope and numerical verification scope are
unchanged. The two qualifications require no new integral or positive gain.

1. The positive upper input has an absolute error

The sufficient Kpos statement is, for each required fixed nonnegative
geometric family of unsifted mass X_N=O(N/log N),

  count_N <= K C_N X_N/log N + o(H_N),
  H_N=C_N N/log^2 N.

Equivalently, X_N=(J+o(1))N/log N gives count_N<=(KJ+o(1))H_N;
a finite limsup upper mass bound is sufficient. No relative o(X_N)
error is asserted for zero-main-mass or very small families. This is
the positive-product implication proved with the same rho in sections
1--2 of lichtman-current-common-interface-review-20261009.txt.

The actual table induction uses nonnegative finite combinations of
fixed, positive-width, compact marked prime boxes. For every retained
nonzero such state, prime harmonic integration gives

  Theta_Q/H_N -> theta=4 integral sigma(t) dmu(t)/L(t) >0.

Its theta may be extremely small, but is a fixed positive number before
N grows. Consequently o(H_N)/Theta_Q=o(1). This is all that the relative
notation (A-H+o(1))*Theta_Q and (a+h+o(1))*Theta_Q requires on the
retained states. There is no uniformity as the box mass tends to zero,
no shrinking box chosen with N, and no per-individual-prefix claim.
There are finitely many states after the finite compact approximation,
so their errors can be summed and their thresholds maximized.

Empty marked families contribute zero. Genuine boundary families and
zero-normalized-mass pieces are treated with the applicable absolute
o(H_N) bound or the reserved fixed positive approximation error; no
relative table conclusion is obtained by dividing by a vanishing Theta.
A zero-main-mass SWITCH inside a nonzero marked state is also harmless:
its absolute o(H_N) bound is o(Theta_Q). Power-small exceptional masses
are negligible by their elementary fixed-power estimates and C_N>=C_2.

In particular W1's unit quotient is power-small because
alpha-3/s>=1/2 on the fixed positive residual range. This does NOT
delete the unit surfaces in W2 Gamma20/21: their normalized mass can
be positive and they remain explicit positive families in Kpos.

All five outer K feedback costs only need absolute o(H_N) remainders.
Their finite union and the fixed action bank therefore use precisely
this weaker sufficient Kpos statement. The original section 0 should
not be read as requesting a relative-error theorem on arbitrary
zero-main-mass families.

2. W1 must retain its prefix-weighted integral until a uniform bound

Write w_b=sigma(b)/(phi(b) log(Q/b))>=0 and let
I_b=I(s,s0,alpha_b) be the actual switched integral at that prefix.
The finite-family mass formula, before any uniform comparison, is

  X_N=N sum_b w_b I_b + o(N/log N).

One must not replace I_b by an arbitrary representative value of the
marked exponent or set alpha_b to two. The finite W1 bank instead has
the uniform nonnegative envelope

  I_b <= I_hat(s,s0)
       = integral_{1/s0<u1<u2<u3<1/s}
          Omega((2-u1-u2-u3)/u2)/(u1*u2^2*u3) du1 du2 du3,

where Omega(v)=max(C,1/v) for v>=1, C=70893/125000. For these actions
s>=2.4, so the displayed base argument is at least 2s-3>1. On actual
nonunit support alpha_b>=2 and omega(actual argument)<=Omega(base
argument). The uniform bound follows from a nonincreasing upper
envelope, not monotonicity of omega or equality at alpha=2. Ordinary
compact approximation controls the harmless within-box variation of
levels and endpoint labels before the arithmetic limit.

Nonnegativity of w_b now permits
X_N <= N I_hat sum_b w_b + o(N/log N). The normalization is

  Theta_Q=4 li(N) sum_b C(bN) w_b,
  C_N<=C(bN).

Combining the absolute positive upper theorem in part 1 with
Theta_Q/H_N->theta>0 yields

  count_N/Theta_Q
   <= (K/4) [N/(li(N) log N)]
      [C_N sum_b w_b I_b / sum_b C(bN) w_b] + o(1)
   <= K I_hat/4 + o(1).

If no uniform bound has yet been chosen, the first line with the
nonnegative weighted integral is the correct version. One may also
retain its normalized weighted average; pulling out a varying I_b
without justification is invalid. The W1 twofold pointwise inequality
then divides the switched cost by two and gives K I_hat/8. The finite
action source uses this uniform mass bound (or its outward enclosure),
so its numerical baseline is unaffected by the notation correction.

The same principle applies to the W2 positive costs: keep all actual
nonunit and unit masses, take their proved uniform envelopes over
actual alpha>=2, and divide once by the W2 coefficient five. It does
not add any new K term or re-add a kernel saving.

3. Scope

The downstream implication for c=1.95867358 needs only the absolute

qualification consumes a positive part of the numerical margin: the
