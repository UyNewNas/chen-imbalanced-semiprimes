\subsection{Complete independent reconstruction of the mesh-$800$ fourth cover}

This review uses the previously frozen independent fourth-chord
implementation, originally written separately from the numerical
author implementation. Its geometry and integral functions accept
arbitrary rational boxes; only its old driver fixed mesh $400$ and
old output filenames. The new driver instead enumerates mesh $800$
at $w=19/125$, with the exact terminal partial interval and the same
depth-four history subdivision. No old source or output is edited.

The independent history enumeration produces $294115$ leaves. Every
one of the author's $83807$ positive parent boxes must occur in this
set exactly once. Their multiplicity factors are reconstructed as
the product of factorials of repeated outer bins. The numerical
prefilter is not imported or reproduced: any chosen finite subfamily
may omit nonnegative ideal fourth gain. Only the actually contributing
parent boxes require integral reconstruction.

On every saved positive parent the independent engine reconstructs
the symmetric third-cap lower and upper bounds, its affine majorant,
all possible orders of old unselected slots, whole-box selected gates,
candidate last-variable partitions and range cutoffs. It separately
computes the lower eight-corner new term and upper Jensen old term,
their exact ordinary $H(u)=u\bar F(u)$ primitive integrals, the
constant-corner fallback and the unique-lowest-bin ordering strip.
The core contributes the maximum of its two alternative bounds;
only the disjoint strip is added. The same ideal $C_3$ and full
all-mask $C_4$ remain the target.

The ordinary delay table at mesh $65536$, maximum $32$, and $112$
fixed-point bits is an explicitly shared, previously audited
dependency. Logarithms and interval operations use the independent
$112$-bit, $64$-term backend. No author chord, Jensen, slice or
prefilter implementation is imported by the reconstruction. This
is a complete new execution of a previously independently authored
engine, not a claim that a third numerical engine has been written.

Differences in outward arithmetic may make individual reconstructed
floors smaller than the author's corresponding floors. The final
JSON therefore records both totals, every-parent comparison counts,
the total signed difference, and a complete new witness containing
all independently reconstructed components. The independently
computed lower total is the value proposed for use, whether or not
it contains the author's total. An output labelled as a successful
reconstruction does not imply domination of every author floor.

This result replaces the previous mesh-$400$ lower bound for the
same $I_4(h)$; their overlapping integrals cannot be added. Its
increase contributes one quarter as much to the $L_2$ occurrence
of the complete Chen coefficient. The previous same-policy third
and fifth inputs remain compatible. One common finite causal
approximation and the original ordinary-density and signed-atom
distribution inputs are still necessary; numerical reconstruction
alone establishes none of those analytic theorems.
