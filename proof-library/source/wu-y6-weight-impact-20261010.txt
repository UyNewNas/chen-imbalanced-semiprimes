1. Conclusions and independence

The weight-domain issue concerns the conjunction of RECTANGULAR Y6 and CONTRACTED Y11
printed in Wu 2008, Lemma 2.2. The current project instead proves a weight
with rectangular Y6 and FULL Y11. These are different weights. The known
correction therefore does not, by itself, invalidate either the historical
manuscript's full-Y11 weight or the currently selected full-Y11 weight.

There is a precise repair, stronger than just a numerical observation:
if R6 denotes the positive pair terms removed by the product cut, and R11
the negative four-prefix terms removed by contracting Y11, then R11<=R6
pointwise off the already separated squareful exception. Consequently
W_corrected = W_full - R6 + R11 <= W_full <= 4*1_{Omega(n)<=2}.
The proof is given below and includes the product-cut boundary.

The claim that ordinary f=0 alone resolves every possible numerical effect
would be wrong. The true double-sieve lower correction h is not declared
zero below two in Wu's definition. However, the current project CHOOSES
zero for its lower h insertion below two and uses a separately licensed
level V, not Wu's fixed level 1/2. A positive lower contribution in the
classically deleted strip can therefore be legitimate for the current
full-Y11 weight. We exhibit an exact point of this kind below.

This review does not reprove prime distribution, the table actions, their
recurrence, or the complete numerical integrations; it does not restore
or change any global K,c,b. I participated in the project's earlier
full-weight and typed-state reviews. This is a new primary-source reading
and independently written exact diagnostic, not an independent authorship
claim for those earlier proofs. The checker imports no project code.

3. A general pointwise repair and an exact negative control

Suppose n is squarefree and N^a-rough. Write its factors q1<q2<... .
If an ordered tuple (p1,p2,p3,p4) contributes to full Y11, then p1=q1
and p2=q2: otherwise a missing factor less than p2 survives in n/b,
contradicting P^-(n/b)>=p2. Its last two slots satisfy
N^a<=p3<N^h<=p4<N^d, so (p3,p4) contributes to the rectangular Y6.
For a tuple removed by the contraction, p3p4>=N^B, and that pair is
removed by the corrected Y6 domain. The map is injective because q1,q2
are forced by n. All other factors remain N^a-rough. This proves R11<=R6.
Equality at p3p4=N^B lies in BOTH removed sets (the retained condition
is strict). For the unit or fewer than four factors R11=0. Squareful
integers are handled by the existing O_a(N^(1-a)) exception separately.

Deleting positive Y6 alone also preserves any already valid full-Y11
inequality, but that elementary observation does not justify contracting
a negative Y11. The injection above is exactly what is needed for the
simultaneous change.

At Wu's printed parameters a=100/1327, h=25/206, d=1/2-3a, use the
strict exponent pattern (.08,.09,.12,.27,.40), total .96>1-a.
The independently enumerated full vector is
 (Y1,...,Y11)=(1,0,4,4,3,3,0,0,0,0,1).
The full weight is 0. Since .12+.27=.39>B, the printed contraction
deletes its sole Y11 and leaves weight 1 on Omega=5.
The corrected Y6 deletes all three pairs (.08,.27), (.09,.27), (.12,.27),
since even .35>B, leaving weight -2. All Y7,Y8,Y9 are zero after their ACTUAL residual cutoffs,
not merely because of a count shortcut. The checker enumerates them.
The pattern is above the customary n<N^(1-a) negligible range and
does not use a square or any threshold equality.

This is a failure of the pointwise extension of the printed weight,
not a constructed asymptotic counterexample to the aggregate theorem
over n=N-P. We have not proved primality of such a shifted P or a
positive-density family of counterexamples. Wu's printed proof p.373
shrinks a negative triple-sum range before concluding -Y10-Y11; that
step needs the corresponding positive loss. The repaired inequality
can instead be deduced from the full-range case proof and the injection.

4. Ordinary positivity and what it does NOT prove about h

At the classical endpoint nu=1/2, s0=(1/2-t-u)/a. The corrected
triangle t+u<B is exactly s0>2. The ordinary normalized numerator
a_lin(s)=s*f(s)/(2e^gamma) is zero for s<=2, as explicitly printed
on pp.374 and 384. Consequently integrating that ORDINARY function
over the original rectangle or its corrected triangle gives the same
ordinary contribution, up to a null boundary. No negative continuation
of log(s-1) should be used as an identity for a_lin below two.

The statement h(s)=0 below two does not follow. Wu defines h on [1,10],
states h>=0 and increasing on [1,2] (p.375), and gives h(2)>0 in Table 2
(p.376). The finite definitions and a(s)+h(s) require their own domains.
Thus an arbitrary import of h to the discarded strip would need removal
or a different correct pointwise weight. The upper correction H has the
opposite sign and belongs to different count occurrences; it does not
repair a mistaken positive Y6 contribution.

For this specific printed 0.899 computation, p.386 bounds G6 by displayed
integrals STARTING at sieve variable u=2. These displayed positive terms
lie within the retained triangle. Hence the Y6 domain correction ALONE
does not prove that Wu's displayed final numerical lower bound decreases.
We have not revalidated those tables or integrations, or the complete
published proof; this is a limited observation about the shown domains.

There is a separate printed-use mismatch worth retaining: Proposition 4.3
on p.378 requires phi4<1/4, whereas p.382 assigns both Y6(a) and (b)
to that proposition with large-prime endpoints 1/2-2h and 1/2-3a,
both greater than 1/4 at the printed parameters. The U_k definition's
largest-slot square condition also requires that endpoint below 1/4
at level 1/2. No missing extension is supplied in the passages read.
This is an incompletely audited, separate applicability question, not
part of the Y6 correction or a conclusion that the 0.899 result is false.
No search for a possible extension is undertaken here. No corrected
1.9728 constant is supplied or inferred in this review.
