\subsection{One compatible density construction for the current product constant}

selected-subproduct and signed-distribution interfaces. The number
\[

\paragraph{Why the different ranks can be added.}
For the same four successive trees, the exact densities satisfy
\[
 V_3-V_6=(V_3-V_4)+(V_4-V_5)+(V_5-V_6).
\]
The fourth and fifth/sixth prefix cylinders need not be disjoint
across ranks. Their nesting is handled by this telescoping identity.
Disjointness is required within each same-rank certificate cover,
and has been checked there. All residual-density normalizations
are the same: $2e^{-\gamma}F=4\bar F$ and likewise for $f$.
Ordered prime prefixes are integrated once with $d\mu_r$;
the certificates introduce no extra factorial or family mass.

For a new branch at rank $r$, all initial complete squares and
all remaining-selected squares are checked below its endpoint.
After the selected slots have occurred, every later remaining
square is exactly $S_{j-1}+2t_j$. An admitted odd cube bounds its
own square; at an even rank ordering gives
$S_{j-1}+2t_j\le S_{j-2}+3t_{j-1}$. Initial gates therefore
propagate through every complete descendant. Along the constructed
path a later change increases the cap; it never reinstates the
superseded smaller later-cube tests. First exclusions stay odd.
This verifies the support and factorization hypotheses of the
existing finite-atom compilation rather than merely bounding the
sum of selected exponents.

\paragraph{One common finite buffered construction.}
Choose the base first/third approximation and all fourth labels
together. Refine by every fifth and sixth label and by the finitely
many active affine branches. Trim ordering faces, inherited
membership boundaries, strict gate surfaces, and positive-gap
boundaries. On the remaining compact pieces choose increasing
constant caps strictly below their selected endpoints, above the
inherited caps and all required gates. On deleted upgrade strips
revert to the inherited rule; never delete an even node arbitrarily.
An identically tied endpoint uses a fixed tie rule; an identically
zero strict gate contributes no active branch.

All new coordinates have fixed positive lower bounds
($3/200$ at rank four, $1/50$ in the later witnesses).
Their density kernels are bounded after the indicated trimming,
and their limiting integrals are controlled on compact sets.
The only improper small-prime tail remains the original third
correction; its existing uniform $O(h\log^2(1/h))$ majorant
is unchanged. Ordinary upper and lower residual-density
\emph{equalities}, rather than differences of one-sided estimates,
evaluate the exact signed identities at fixed cutoffs.

Fix an error allowance smaller than one tenth of $K-E$ for each
base approximation, each of the three refinements, and the finite
boxing/small-prime errors. Common refinements may increase the
number of labels but it stays finite. After freezing these data,
choose the presieve precision and then the finer atom grid as in
the compilation lemma. Their constants may depend on the chosen
finite tree. Take $N\to\infty$ only after these choices.
The dominated limits show that some such common choice has total
error below $K-E$. There is no $N$-dependent adaptive cap choice,
no infinite iteration, and no demand for error estimates uniform
in an unbounded number of labels.
