\subsection{Parallel slanted slabs for fourth-node restoration}
\label{sec:fourth-restoration-slanted-20261009}

This certificate uses the pre-own-cube restoration action of
\texttt{lower-fourth-own-cube-restoration-20261009.tex} and preserves
every old third ancestor.  It changes numerical coverage of its
old-exit domain, not the previously retained fourth/fifth policy.
The original whole-box restoration total is an alternative on the
same domain and must be replaced, not added.

Put $\nu=249/448$, $h=19/125$, $\Lambda=153/256$ and
$r_0=(\nu-h)/9$.  For fixed $n\in\{400,800\}$ consider
\[
 B=\prod_{j=1}^4[\ell_j,u_j],\qquad
 (\ell_1,\ell_2,\ell_3,\ell_4)=(i,j,k,l)/n,
 \quad u_j=\ell_j+1/n,
\]
where $\lceil nr_0\rceil\le l<k<j<i$ and
$i+1\le\lfloor nh\rfloor$.  These boxes are ordered without
factorials.  Faces have zero integral and may consistently be made
half open.  Only boxes with $u_1+3u_2<\nu$ are used.
For
\[
 v(b)=\min\{(1+b)/2,(32-34b)/39,57/64-3b/2\},
\]
compute an upper bound $U_3$ for the old ideal third cap by taking
the maximum of $\nu$, all seven nonempty selected-$v$ upper bounds
on the first three coordinates, and $\Lambda-3\ell_3/8$.
This includes inactive branches and all three $W$ upper planes,
so it is an upper bound rather than a claim of equality.
For each $v$ sum interval its minimum is an endpoint minimum and its
maximum also checks the peak $25/128$, whose height is $\Lambda$.
Every old actual buffered third cap remains at most $U_3$.

Use the integer common denominator
\[
 D=\operatorname{lcm}(448,256,78n,64n,8n),\quad d=D/n.
\]
For each mask $I\subset\{1,2,3,4\}$ containing $4$, let
$c_I=\inf_Bv(\sum_{j\in I}t_j)-D^{-1}$.
Keep it only if
\[
 c_I>U_3,\qquad
 c_I>\max_{j\le4}(S_{j-1,+}+2u_j),\qquad c_I>G_{I,+}.
\]
The full and selected-remaining gates are checked on the entire
original box, using their upper affine endpoints.  Choose one
largest surviving $c=c_I$.  This choice is licensed even when the
old or new fourth cube fails on part of the box.  Among fixed caps
passing these gates, a larger cap has no smaller restored-domain
integrand, since $\bar f$ is increasing in the range proved below.
No future prime or error estimate enters this decision.

\paragraph{Disjoint clipped slabs and their integrands.}
Write $r=t_4$ and $A=t_1+t_2+t_3+3r$.  Let $A_-,A_+$ be its
box endpoints and set
\[
 a_0=\max(A_-,U_3+D^{-1}),\qquad
 a_m=\min(A_+,c-D^{-1}).
\]
If $a_m\le a_0$, discard the box.  Otherwise fix an integer
$m\ge1$, put $a_j=a_0+j(a_m-a_0)/m$, and use the disjoint
slabs $E_j=B\cap\{a_{j-1}<A<a_j\}$.  Their bounding walls have
zero volume.  In every retained slab
\[
 A>U_3+D^{-1}\ge C_3+D^{-1},\qquad A<c-D^{-1}.
\]
Thus the old fourth node was rejected, the new node is admitted,
and both comparisons have a fixed positive buffer.  No previously
counted retained fourth or fifth domain is included.  The additional
raw density has the single ordinary lower residual at level $c$;
there is no old residual term to subtract.

The ordinary argument satisfies
\[
 s=\frac{c-S_4}{r}=2+\frac{c-A}{r}
 \ge s_j:=2+\frac{c-a_j}{u_4}>2.
\]
All actual $s$ and the displayed lower endpoints are less than $3$.
Indeed $A>U_3\ge\nu$, $c\le\Lambda$ and $r\ge r_0$, whence
\[
 s-2\le\frac{\Lambda-\nu}{r_0}
 =\frac{84375}{90452}<1.
\]
Consequently $\bar f(s)=\log(s-1)/s$ and $\bar f'(s)>0$ apply
without a delay table.  The exact lower estimate for a layer is
\[
 \frac{4\bar f(s_j)}{u_1u_2u_3u_4^2}\operatorname{Vol}(E_j).
\]
This is a bound against ordinary Cartesian volume.  It makes no
independence assertion about logarithmic weights after clipping.

\paragraph{Exact volume and outward arithmetic.}
After scaling by $n$, the four weighted widths for $A-A_-$ are
$(1,1,1,3)$.  More generally, in integer units with common denominator
$D'=mD$ and width $w=md$, define
\[
 T(x)=\sum_{e\in\{0,1\}^4}(-1)^{|e|}
       \bigl(x-w(e_1+e_2+e_3+3e_4)\bigr)_+^4.
\]
Then $\operatorname{Vol}(B\cap\{A<t\})=
T(D'(t-A_-))/(72D'^4)$: the factor is
$4!\,(1\cdot1\cdot1\cdot3)=72$.
Grouping equal offsets gives coefficients
$(1,-3,3,-2,3,-3,1)$ at offsets $0,w,\ldots,6w$.
The program checks this against the sixteen-term expression;
$T(6w)=72w^4$ and $T(3w)=36w^4$.
Each layer uses the exact difference of its two CDF numerators.
No numerical polytope library or floating volume is used.

For $s_j=a/b$ put $z=(a-2b)/a\in(0,1/3)$.
With $Q_L=2^{112}$ define
\[
 L_j=\sum_{q=0}^{7}\left\lfloor
       \frac{2Q_L(a-2b)^{2q+1}}{(2q+1)a^{2q+1}}\right\rfloor.
\]
The positive atanh expansion gives $L_j/Q_L\le\log(s_j-1)$.
If $V_j$ is the CDF-numerator difference and
$P=(i+1)(j+1)(k+1)(l+1)^2$, the saved $Q=2^{80}$ floor is
\[
 F_j=\left\lfloor
       \frac{n b L_j Q V_j}{18w^4 a Q_L P}\right\rfloor.
\]
This is exactly the preceding lower estimate using
$u_1u_2u_3u_4^2=P/n^5$ and $D'=nw$.
The finite positive-series truncation and every rounding are downward.

\paragraph{Replacement, finite compilation and scope.}
The program recomputes every previously saved whole-box floor at
the same mesh and verifies its box, mask, cap and integer value.
For each original box it records both the old complete-box strategy
and the new complete slab strategy, and chooses
\[
 F_B=\max\{F_{B,\mathrm{old}},\ \sum_jF_j\}.
\]
Only that chosen strategy is compiled; overlapping alternatives
are never added.  The new total is $\Delta=Q^{-1}\sum_B F_B$ and
replaces the old restoration total.  If a previous global assembly
already included the old total, the only extra Chen coefficient
is $(\Delta-\Delta_{\rm old})/4$.  Mesh-$400$ and mesh-$800$
certificates are also alternatives, and not disjoint additions.

Every chosen cap, buffer, box and slab is fixed before the asymptotic
limit.  The new affine walls permit finite common refinement with
the preserved old third policy; equality walls may be removed and
the remaining compact inner domains approximated with arbitrarily
small density loss.  The minimum recorded history and gate slack
fixes how small the old root buffer may be chosen.  First-exit parity,
signed-atom compilation, descendant selected gates and the raw
ordinary residual equality are precisely the inputs of the existing
restoration proof, rather than newly inferred distribution theorems.
No restored node receives a new fifth refinement.

The numerical output is an author certificate and records its own
source, witness and inherited-proof hashes.  A second implementation
must independently replay every selected box and layer before this
component is promoted to independently certified status.  This note
does not select a new global $c$, $b$ or prime-representation margin.
