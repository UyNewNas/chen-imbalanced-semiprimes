1. Definitions that keep the exact mean and the target distinct

Write T for the even target, x for a COMPLETE distributed integer scale,
and q for a complete arithmetic modulus. For sequences on n~N,m~M, x=NM,
define
  Delta_q(a;alpha,beta)
   =sum_(n,m) alpha_n beta_m
        [1_(nm=a mod q)-1_(gcd(nm,q)=1)/phi(q)].             (1)
The mean in (1) is the exact coprime mass. It is not total mass/phi(q),
nor a singular-series approximation. Throughout the application a=T.
It is not T divided by a marked prefix; the prefix belongs in the modulus.

For k labelled original prime slots and a weight W_T, define
  E_q(T;W_T)=sum_(p1,...,pk) W_T(p1,...,pk)
      [1_(product p_i=T mod q)-1_(gcd(product p_i,q)=1)/phi(q)]. (2)
The labelled multiplicities and any fixed scalar are identical on both
sides. One may sum finitely many such families. The target may influence
fixed cutoffs, geometric boxes, marked labels and coefficient sequences,
but all stipulated bounds must have constants uniform in that influence.

2. Local medium convolution lemma: the minimal class actually needed

Fix theta=7/32, sigma>0 sufficiently small, 0<=kappa<=sigma/100 and c0>0.
Let N,M,Q1,Q2,Q3>=1, x=NM, c0*x<=|a|<=x^(1+kappa), and require
  Q1 < N*x^(-sigma),
  N^2 Q2 Q3^2 < x^(1-8sigma),
  |a|^theta N Q1 Q2^5 Q3^2 < x^(2-15sigma),
  N^(1+2theta) Q1 Q2^(5-2theta) Q3^(2+2theta)
                                    < x^(2-15sigma).       (3)
Fixed factors in dyadic endpoint conventions are paid by strict margins.
Unit modulus groups may be delta_1, at a bounded nominal scale; they are
not asserted to be SW sequences or to have positive growing length.

The n-sequence alpha is one-bounded, supported in one fixed subinterval
of [N,2N] and on Pminus(n)>z0=x^(1/(loglog x)^3). It has FULL auxiliary
SW: for every A>0 and every k,q>=1, uniformly for gcd(b,q)=1,
  |sum_(n=b mod q,(n,k)=1)alpha_n
       -phi(q)^(-1)sum_((n,kq)=1)alpha_n|
             <= C_A N tau(k)^C0 (log x)^(-A),              (4)
with one fixed C0 and constants uniform in the coefficient family.
In the actual HB class C0=1. Only small q is used in the character
mean-square proof, but the actual rough-SW derivation supplies (4) for
all q. The sequence alpha cannot vary with the modulus being summed.

The complementary beta is supported in [M,2M] and satisfies
|beta_m|<=tau(m)^B for a FIXED B. It need not be SW or one-bounded.
For i=1,2,3 let gamma_i be one-bounded complex sequences on q_i~Qi,
supported on gcd(q_i,a)=1. They are separate one-variable sequences.
Then, relative to the standard inputs listed in section 8,
  |sum_(q1,q2,q3) gamma_1(q1)gamma_2(q2)gamma_3(q3)
                             Delta_(q1q2q3)(a;alpha,beta)|
                       <<_A x (log x)^(-A)                (5)
for every A>0. Constants depend on the fixed coefficient and margin data,
not on a or endpoints within their stated uniform family.

This is a signed, factorized-modulus estimate. It does not bound the sum
of absolute discrepancies up to the full high level, nor allow an arbitrary
divisor-bounded coefficient rho(q) in place of the three factors. Grouping
the three factors gives a divisor majorant only for auxiliary tail estimates.
There is no supremum over independently chosen alpha_q or beta_q.

The actual alpha is one individual rough 1 or mu HB slot, with individual
interval, normalized logarithms, and a Mellin twist |t|<=log^J x.
The explicit rough-SW proof derives (4), uniformly in the chart cutoff
  f(x)<=Zchart<=x^(2/(loglog x)^3), f(x)=x^(1/(loglog x)^3),
and all auxiliary integers. A larger fixed chart projector is retained
literally: support above Zchart implies the source support above z0.
Internal splitting of q2,q3 at z0 does not change that HB projector.
Alternatively splitting at Zchart changes only fixed constants in the
smooth-part bound. These are different operations on different integers.

The other HB slots have normalized convolution bound d_J<=tau^(J-1),
J<=6k_star-1. Thus the actual application needs ONLY beta truncation:
beta'=beta*1_(tau(m)<=log^C x), followed by log^(BC) normalization.
Alpha and (4) are unchanged. For nm=v!=a the deleted coefficient has
bound log^(-C)x*tau(v)^(B+2), and summing moduli costs a fixed power of
tau(|v-a|). The nonzero shifted-moment estimate gives x*log^(E-C)x,
where E is independent of C. The exact v=a is isolated at first-moment
cost Dtot*x^o(1), Dtot<=x^(1-delta); no tau(0) is used. Its coprime mean
uses sum |rho(q)|/phi(q)<<log^O(1)x. Choose C first, then the one-bounded
theorem's logarithmic exponent to pay normalization. Therefore (5) for
the actual HB family does not require a separate SW-after-alpha-truncation
theorem. The broader frozen divisor-extension lemma remains an optional
extension, not a hidden necessary premise of this minimal route.

3. Why the local proof supplies (5) with the claimed normalization

Discard smooth parts exceeding y0=x^(1/loglog x) by the weighted Rankin
and primitive Shiu estimate; its divisor order is fixed. On each dyadic

  Lambda_d(w)=sum_(st=d,uv=w) gamma_2(us)gamma_3(vt)
                     1_(all fixed intervals/rough-smooth conditions).
This coefficient is independent of q1. The number of length blocks is
polylogarithmic. Individual d values are NOT separately summed after a
merely logarithmic theorem; they stay together in preliminary dispersion.

The four inequalities (3) imply N,M>=x^(sigma/3),
Q1 D<=N*x^(-sigma/3), and Q1 D Rcal<=x^(1-sigma/3) eventually.
They provide the preliminary e=1 margins, not just a formal appeal to a
fixed-residue result. Delete nm=a from the original congruence indicator
before Cauchy; leave the exact coprime mean unchanged. The original
first-moment deletion is O(Q1 D Rcal*x^o(1)). Nonzero shifted moments
control roughness/large-divisor cutoffs. Restore S2's single-zero terms
only immediately before its one-congruence Poisson step; restore S3's
single-zero terms only after n1!=n2. Their variance cost is Rcal*N*x^o(1),
giving x^(1-sigma/6+o(1)) after outer Cauchy in this safe subrange.

Let ell=q1*d, Y=Q1*D and eta_r=Lambda_d(r). The exact principal-term
kernel is
  c_ell(r,s)=phi(ell*r*s)/(ell*r*s*phi(ell*r)*phi(ell*s)).
It is a positive finite-unit Gram kernel. On the clean CRT set,
phi(ell)c_ell(r,s)=1/(ell*r*s), exactly. The three terms S1-2Re S2+S3
therefore cancel their common mean and leave the NONNEGATIVE variance P;
they do not simply cancel to zero. Own restrictions (n,r)=1 remain.
For fixed r, apply auxiliary BDH to alpha*1_(n,r)=1/tau(r)^C0.
Its paid factor tau(r)^(2C0) is averaged, not placed in a uniform constant.
For a sufficiently large fixed B the bound has the form
  P << M log^2(x)/(Y Rcal) sum_(r~Rcal) tau(r)^(2B)
             sum_(ell<=8Y) tau(ell)^(2B+1) V_r(ell)
    << M N^2/(Y log^A x).                                 (6)
Here V_r uses the exact coprime mean. Alpha^(r) is independent of ell;
fix r before its full modulus sum. Multiplicity of ell=q1*d costs tau(ell),
and the remaining r-divisor mean cancels the displayed 1/Rcal up to logs.
BDH follows from primitive multiplicative large sieve plus (4), with the
induced-character coprimality filter kept. It is not an additional
beyond-half distribution assumption.

The nonzero-frequency terms enter as
  |S(a)| << x/log^A x + M sqrt(DQ1)log^C x
                           (sqrt(|E1|)+sqrt(|E2|)),         (7)
besides the already power-small exact-product restoration error.
The explicit E1 completion/Weil calculation gives
  |Z_E1|^2 << x^o(1) N^4/Q^2
          [NQ/x+NQ^(5/2)R^3/x^2+N^2QR/x^2]
in its fixed-d variables. Its zero Ramanujan term and ell=0 subcase are

gives |E1|<<N^2/(DQ1)*x^(-sigma/2).

For E2 keep q=q1 and fixed d; E2=sum_(d~D)E3(a;d)/d. Exact reciprocity
retains e(ah/(q d r1 r2 n1)), whose five-coordinate variation costs
x^(5kappa+o(1)). It is paid ONCE. The coprime coloring, two independent

The primitive S=1 spectral return applies with

The return retains the large-a factor 1+|a|/x in its natural L51 bound.
At source margin 3sigma/4, the explicit ledger, with kappa<=sigma/100,

Uniformly in fixed d, summing 1/d=O(1) then paying D=x^o(1) yields

Inserting E1 and (8) into (7) gives their x^(1-sigma/4) contribution.

4. Proposition for the actual compiled prime-product families

Fix a_star>0, k_star, a finite policy and its ancestor/marked labels,
finite approximation tolerances, fixed rough exponent omega>0 and beta
parameters, and a fixed complete modulus ceiling T^Theta with Theta<3/5.
All these choices precede T->infinity. The policies may give a very small
fixed positive a_star, but may not send it to zero with T.

For each T let W_T be a signed or nonnegative smooth geometric weight on
at most k_star labelled original primes p_i>=c*T^a_star, product p_i<=C*T.
It has no arbitrary arithmetic filter on an original prime or HB slot.
After original-chart and HB changes of variables its finite mixed Euler
derivative/L1 separation norm is bounded by C_W log^J T with fixed C_W,J.
Individual interval endpoints are allowed. A genuinely coupled sharp
geometric indicator is not part of this smooth signed statement.
The exact smooth original-group partition below gives that norm for the
actual smooth approximants; it is not an extra unverified hard cutoff.

Let rho_T be the SAME compiled signed modulus coefficient in the count
and in the exact mean, supported on gcd(q,T)=1 and q<=T^Theta. Require the
following actual finite coefficient classes, not just support membership:

(a) Low rough ranks and classical states have |rho_T(q)|<=C_F with C_F
fixed, and their COMPLETE support after beta/endpoint/local conversion
is <=x^(1/2-epsilon_L) for some fixed epsilon_L>0 on all near-target blocks.

(b) Every high-rank atom is a fixed-scalar labelled rough prime convolution
with a one-bounded small-prime beta factor, retaining global rough
squarefreeness. Interval labels first undergo a common disjoint half-open
refinement. All original multiplicities, including marked/residual colors,
are kept. For each Type II length block a partition of its slots is chosen
once on the WHOLE upper-endpoint box. On removing the separately bounded
square-collision remainder, the resulting atom is an exact finite sum of
three one-bounded convolution factors times fixed scalars. The number and
total absolute scalar mass are bounded in terms of the fixed family.

(c) For each medium HB block those group lengths satisfy (3). This is
ensured by the actual full-square and selected-complement tests, or the
repaired W initialization/first-failure rule, applied to upper endpoints
with the reserved support buffer. If the physical cap is T^ell and
qscale=log_x T, the factorization parameter is qscale*ell+2zeta, not
qscale*ell; it must stay inside its source license. The local tent uses
alpha>=log_x T and its full three affine bounds. A mask sum alone is not
a license. The partition may depend on T and the Type II LENGTH, but
does not vary with individual arithmetic tuples or observed errors.

(d) After local conversion the complete high-rank modulus support has a
fixed bound x^Theta_loc with Theta_loc<3/5; the one/two-long gaps and the
low-rank gap in (a) are retained. All outer weights and coefficient
families have the uniform bounds just specified.

Then for every A>0, uniformly for all sufficiently large even T,
  sum_(q,(q,T)=1) rho_T(q) E_q(T;W_T) <<_A T/log^A T.       (PP)
Its constant depends only on fixed family data and uniform norm bounds.
It is a signed discrepancy estimate with ABSOLUTE error. It does not
assert an error relative to a possibly zero or tiny unsifted mass.

For a disjoint label alphabet, a slot convolution has multiplicity

on its actual support. Normalize group j by product_I m_(I,j)! and retain
the scalar product_j product_I m_(I,j)! / product_I m_I! externally.
Each group is <=1 on all integers. The older group-rank factorial is an
equivalent exact normalization with a larger fixed external scalar.
A raw marked sigma has its existing multiplicity; only the ordered
residual slots get their original repeated-label denominator. This same
scalar is present in the count and in Theta. Nothing is silently divided
out on just one side. Global rough collisions are not equivalent to
within-group squarefreeness: they cost
  (T+T^Theta) T^(-omega+o(1)),
paid against a fixed power gap. Beta attaches to one group because the
small/rough factorization is unique. It is one-bounded, not asserted to
be positive; signs select beta+ or beta- in the separate pointwise compiler.

on [1/2,2]. It is an identity in BOTH terms of (2). Do not retain the older
hard indicator R_i<=product(HB group)<=2R_i after this replacement.

  X^(3zeta)<=U<=X^(1/3+zeta/4),
which lies in [x^(2zeta),x^(1/3+zeta/2)] eventually. A fixed chart rule
chooses a single medium slot and the whole-box modulus partition. Apply

If there is no medium slot, a group containing only short slots has at

Thus every original group

6. Error and common-threshold ledger

Choose the complete-product trim before applying local distribution:
v<2T^(1-kappa0). Then v!=T for large T. Fixed divisor multiplicities and
the sum over q|T-v give T^(1-kappa0+o(1)); the exact mean costs only
fixed logarithmic factors. On retained blocks
  c1*T^(1-kappa0)<=x<=C1*T,
so c0*x<=T<=x^(1+kappa) eventually when kappa0/(1-kappa0)<kappa.
This is the complete product, not the residual quotient of a marked state.

Fourier tails are summed AFTER the modulus sum. The nonzero shifted
moment gives x*log^E x times their uniform approximation error; the exact
v=T contributes at most T^(Theta+o(1)) times that error. Never write
tau(0), and do not use final rough parity to skip an intermediate equality.
Proper original prime powers cost T^(1-a_star/2+o(1)), plus the separately
isolated exact-product term. Rough modulus collisions have the bound in
section 5. Each of these has a fixed power reserve.

First fix the finite geometric family, its smoothing tolerances, actual
compiler and positive support reserves. Fix the common small parameters
so every local (3), low-rank and long-slot gap is positive; source sigma
and moving-range kappa are independent parameters, not the same epsilon.
Fix the finite derivative orders, divisor orders and frequency cutoff
power. Then choose requested SW/distribution logarithmic exponents to
pay ALL fixed polylogarithmic chart counts and Fourier total variations.
The complement divisor order depends on k_star, not on those exponents.
Finally take one sufficiently large T threshold for the uniform family.

Growing O(log^J T) HB charts do not become an unbounded collection of
uncontrolled constants: the individual estimates are uniform by their
norm hypotheses, and the counted total norm is paid explicitly. Atom
count/rank may be large but is fixed after the rough cutoff omega is
fixed. No assumption of uniformity as omega or a_star tends to zero is
made. The separate common-parameter review checks this ordering for
ordinary tails as well as enhanced compact labels. One possible hierarchy
is sigma=zeta/2000, kappa=sigma/200, kappa0<kappa/[4(1+kappa)], with zeta
further below every fixed licence, low-rank and complete-support reserve.
Coarse caps and their strict licence reserves are fixed before omega;
then rank J is fixed, and the common endpoint width tau is made small
enough to pay O((J+2)tau). Ordinary-tail full-square inequalities may
remain structurally weak: their endpoint inflation is paid by the
external licence reserve. The proposition requires the resulting
whole-box inequalities, not an invented strict margin at every raw
boundary point. This hierarchy does not supply a missing compiler or
density identity.

7. Exact downstream boundary: what PP supplies and does not supply

(PP) applies to the actual positive upper tree, ordinary lower compiler,
and finite marked H/h descendants WHEN their stated atom/support data
are supplied as in sections 4--5. Each occurrence may have its own rho;
count and exact mean must use that identical rho. The latest policy uses
finite430 only in U3/U4 and retains finite248 elsewhere. Finitely many
generations leave a fixed positive minimum prime exponent and finite
maximum slot count, even if the minimum is extremely small. No new
distribution assertion uniform in an infinite recursion is needed.

To obtain a positive product theorem one still needs, separately, a
pointwise nonnegative upper majorant and a density bound for that SAME
rho. For large original primes the exact coprime mean can then be replaced
by its unsifted mass with a power-small error; PP alone is not that density
bound. The sufficient final form is
  count <= K C_T X_T/log T + o(H_T), H_T=C_T*T/log^2 T,
with absolute error. Empty/zero-main-mass families are not given a relative
o(X_T) assertion. A retained positive fixed state may divide by Theta_Q
only after Theta_Q/H_T tends to a fixed positive number. The W1 mass must
keep its nonnegative prefix-weighted integral until a uniform bound is
taken; actual alpha is not reset to 2. W2 unit surfaces stay in the positive
mass. These are downstream requirements, not coefficient variants of (5).

Sharp positive geometric counts follow only by fixed-tolerance positive
outer smoothing, then T->infinity, then the tolerance tending to zero.
An arbitrary sharp SIGNED coupled-domain PP theorem is not asserted.
Radial product equalities and unit quotients require their existing
separate treatment. Uniform C_T>=C_2 suffices to compare absolute errors
with H_T; no uniform upper bound on C_T is assumed.

PP does not prove exact raw Rosser density identities, first-exit parity,
the full-Y11 pointwise weight, W1/W2 operator inequalities, finite table
values, quadrature floors, or the final numerical budgets. It neither
uses external Li's c=1.9728 nor restores that result. The separately
identified Wu Y6 correction and the project's full-Y11/no-loss check
are logically outside this distribution proposition.
