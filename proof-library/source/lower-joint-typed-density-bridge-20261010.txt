0. Conclusion and independent contribution

The finite lower interface can be closed relative to three DISTINCT inputs:
(D) literal raw Rosser density equalities and prime harmonic/radial mass;
(P) the restricted moving-target signed distribution proposition, with
    a valid whole-box factorization parameter covering the actual support;
(K) the positive-product upper theorem for each required fixed positive
    cutoff, proved by its own pointwise upper tree and SAME-rho density.
Neither P alone nor a numerical table proves D or K.

This note reconstructs the finite query graph, gives explicit positive
cutoff and error-propagation bounds, and checks that the ordinary joint
approximation and restored fourth nodes can feed that graph. It does not
rerun the table or multidimensional integrals. I participated in earlier
compiler and typed-state audits. The new work is this quantitative finite
composition and the checks below, not a second-author reproof of all those
earlier inputs. No status string is used as a mathematical premise.

2. A quantitative positive-cutoff bound for the actual table graph

For one appended prime x=Lu, the exact transformation is
  L'=L(1-u), alpha'=1+(alpha-1)/(1-u)>=alpha.
For an appended pair Lu,Lv it is
  L'=L(1-u-v), alpha'=1+(alpha-1)/(1-u-v)>=alpha.
The new full square is exactly the corresponding residual square;
after every old selected slot has occurred the selected-remaining test
is the same full square. The inherited W ceiling also remains mandatory.
This verifies the support on the COMPLETE concatenated atom.

bounds. Treating its nested Buchstab step separately only makes this bound

After depth at most440 one may therefore use the uniform conservative

  e_min=min(e0,L_min/6)>0.                                  (1)

3. Finite stability with an explicit coarse operator norm

All feedback measures in the admitted bank are nonnegative. A bound12
for the sum of their masses is sufficient, uniformly in actual alpha:
  Buchstab: integral_1^5 dw/w=log5<2.
  W1: unchanged-prefix weight1 plus half a singleton integral, at most
       1+(1/2) integral_(1/5)^(1/2) du/[u(1-u)]<2.
  W2: unchanged-prefix mass(4+1)/5=1; its three singleton integrals
       contribute at most3log4/5. On each of the four pair domains,
       u,v>=1/5, u,v<=1/2 and1-u-v>=u>=1/5. Thus the kernel is<=125
       and domain area<=(3/10)^2, so their total after division by5
       is at most9. The total is<1+6/5+9<12.                 (2)
The actual domains are smaller, so no endpoint/gate extension is required.
Discarding nonnegative subregions or using downward weights only reduces
these norms. A finite maximum is1-Lipschitz in the uniform norm, since
|max_i a_i-max_i b_i|<=max_i|a_i-b_i|. The unchanged old entry causes no
additional sum. Negative switched costs are fixed base terms at this
stage; they are not counted again as feedback edges.

Here is a precise finite-error lemma. Suppose every primitive ordinary
or positive-product query and every local finite approximation has an
oriented normalized error at most delta in absolute value. Let e_j be
the maximal defect error after j elementary levels. Then
  e_0<=delta, e_(j+1)<=delta+12e_j,
  e_j<=delta*(12^(j+1)-1)/11.                              (3)
The bound follows by induction from positive kernels and (2), including
finite maxima. It also works for two competing upper estimates selected
by a minimum after orienting the error. For any desired top error epsilon
one can choose delta<epsilon/S_440, S_440=(12^441-1)/11.
This is an existence bound, not a request to compute440-generation
analytic error numerically.

For asymptotic arithmetic errors it is even simpler: once the compact
geometric vertices have been fixed, every positive-mass marked vertex has
  Theta_v/H_T -> theta_v>0,
  Theta_v=4 li(T) sum_B sigma_v(B) C(BT)/[phi(B) log(Q/B)].
There are finitely many such v; min theta_v>0. Thus each absolute o(H_T)
K error is o(Theta_v), and finitely many constants and thresholds can be
maximized. Empty or zero-normalized-mass pieces are handled absolutely;
they are never divided by Theta_v. An arbitrary arithmetic sigma_T or a
box shrinking with T would not have this conclusion and is not licensed.

4. Constructing the geometric vertices without changing ancestor caps

For the two ordinary starts first truncate the last active coordinates
below epsilon. Put d0=nu-3h>0, nu=249/448. The old upper arguments have
numerator>=d0; fourth history gives C3-S4>2d0/3. Before a sufficiently
small fifth prime is removed the same reserve remains bounded below.
Using the ordinary asymptotics Fbar(s)-e^(-gamma)/2=O(s^-2) and
e^(-gamma)/2-fbar(s)=O(s^-2), each difference cancels its last-prime
t_r^(-2) singularity. Therefore the unretained tails satisfy
  I_r(t_r<epsilon)=O(epsilon*(1+log^(r-1)(h/epsilon))), r=3,4,5. (4)
The bounds are uniform for a small baseline buffer; choose that buffer
less than d0/2. These are tails of exact density differences, not
differences of two unrelated one-sided bounds.

On the resulting compact set refine the finite cap/branch arrangement.
True nonidentical affine tie walls have zero weighted mass. Identical
functions do not: an open inherit region C4=C3 is kept with its literal
label and can carry a positive fifth improvement. Likewise C3=nu and
C5=C4 are retained. Empty strict action intervals are not actions.

Freeze the third cap on a first-three-coordinate box, BELOW its branch
endpoint. Refine each fourth fibre while retaining that actual third cap,
and test S3+3t4<lambda3. Then refine the fifth fibre while retaining its
actual fourth parent. Do not let a nominal third cap depend on t4 or t5.
A child-boundary projection may fill the parent domain; only the child
boundary is trimmed. For an admissible ideal fourth point, the positive
margin C3-S3-3t4 ensures eventual admission. Its equality set is a graph
in t4 since C3 does not depend on t4, hence is null. Bounded convergence
then controls all three compact telescopes together. Their separate
approximations need not be monotone in the caps.

The same nested construction applies to the finite unrolled table graph.
Use physical appended exponents as coordinates. After a fixed level and
branch are chosen, ordinary/selected histories and table-knot preimages
are finite affine or continuous piecewise-algebraic walls with positive
denominators on the compact set. Refine at their actual rank, retaining
all earlier decisions, full/selected gates and stable ceilings. Smooth
or truncate positive switched leaves separately as in section6. The
finite graph depth, finite bank and fixed cutoffs make this a finite
iteration of ordinary compact approximation. Equation(3) determines how
small each local approximation must be to meet one final tolerance.

A nonidentical table-knot preimage can be trimmed. A structural clamp
s=2 can have positive measure and is kept, using the smaller adjacent
closed-cell value. At an identical fixed comparison argument keep the
closed-cell estimate and its comparison rule, not a discarded wall.
Cutoff=the new smallest marked prime is another structural equality:
future sieve primes remain strictly smaller. An inherited cap is not
replaced by a freshly optimized cap after a later refinement.

6. The exact density bridge and its unit/radial exceptions

The necessary ordinary input is the equality for the SAME raw residual
coefficients,
  V_T^+(T^beta,T^t)/V_T(T^t)=F(beta/t)+o(1), beta/t>1,
  V_T^-(T^beta,T^t)/V_T(T^t)=f(beta/t)+o(1), beta/t>=2.       (6)
The old compiler proves the moving-target reduction as follows. A
surviving positive first-exit term, normalized by V_T(y), equals its
fixed-g0 counterpart times
  product_(q_last<=p<y,p|T) (1-g0(p)) in[0,1].
Other terms vanish. Fixed-rank exit primes have a positive exponent,
so the deletion and product correction tend uniformly to their fixed-g0
limits. The classical convergent cubic Rosser positive-exit series then
sandwiches the full raw density: first take T to infinity at fixed rank,
then let the fixed rank grow. The convergence of that classical series
is an explicit external input, not supplied by PP or finite numerics.
For1<s<3, upper support is EXACTLY unchanged on lowering the cutoff to
R^(1/3); its Euler-product ratio3/s extends(6) from s=3. No lower density
equality below two is used.

Strict residual primes lie below the last marked prime, so
g_T(Pd)=g_T(P)g_T(d). The exact changes at ranks3,4,5 are respectively
  g_T(P3)[V^+(old)-V^+(new)],
  g_T(P4)[V^-(new)-V^-(old)],
  g_T(P5)[V^+(old)-V^+(new)].                              (7)
They are successive changes of ONE causal tree. Insert(6) only after
(7); never subtract two upper bounds or two lower bounds. Euler-product
normalization gives pi(T)V_T(T^t)/H_T=2e^(-gamma)/t+o(1), hence the
factor4 on Fbar/fbar. This normalization alone does not prove(6).

For a fixed nonnegative marked family its geometric mass is
  Theta_Q/H_T ->4 integral sigma(t) dmu(t)/L(t).
The singleton child contributes du/[u(1-u)] and the pair child
du dv/[uv(1-u-v)]. W1's switched integral must stay inside the
nonnegative marked weighted average until its uniform alpha>=2 envelope
has been used. Division by Theta then yields K I_hat/4, and the W1
leading factor two gives K I_hat/8. For W2 the division by five occurs
once after assembling its ordinary and positive switched terms. Actual
alpha may grow down the graph; it is never reset to two.

Canonical rough quotients have finitely many prime factors by(1).
The UNIT quotient in W1 is power-small on its stated bank, but units
in W2 Gamma20/21 have a nonzero limiting mass and are separate positive
prime-product leaves. Eliminating their last logarithmic coordinate
uses derivative one, not a new alpha or Euclidean surface Jacobian.
A set of zero volume in the nonunit exponent coordinates is not thereby
negligible for this distinct unit family.

In any such product family use the actual radial coordinate
y=product(primes)/T. With k prime slots, limiting unsifted mass is
  dt1...dt_(k-1) dy/[t1...t_(k-1)*(1-sum_(i<k)ti)].
On positive cutoffs this has bounded density. A monomial wall with
nonconstant simplex part is null; a critical pure-product wall is a
wall in y and must be smoothed THERE. Fixed radial width tends to zero
after the arithmetic limit. A fixed exponent width about total-product
one would generally have nonvanishing limiting mass and is invalid.
The small y tail has O(eta) mass by the last-prime PNT upper estimate
and bounded reciprocal-prime sums. These facts justify positive outer
approximation before sieving. They do not bound a signed progression
remainder merely from the small mass of its boundary.

8. Limits, findings, and source locations

No new contradiction was found in this finite lower composition after
the auxiliary support-cover qualification in section0. The tempting
shortcuts ruled out by the proof are substantive: finite generations
without compact queries; dropping inherit/clamp/unit strata; adding the
restoration cover to its predecessor; using a raw ideal parent for an
unbuffered child; deriving exact density by subtracting inequalities;
or dividing by a zero normalized marked mass.

The explicit retained obligations are the classical raw cubic Rosser
convergence in(6), the first-exit/sign-sensitive beta compiler, valid
actual whole-box modulus licences including the auxiliary support cover,
the named classical/spectral inputs of restricted PP, the independent
same-rho positive upper theorem, and the outward ordinary/action/table/
functional inequalities. This report joins those inputs and proves the
finite error/quantifier step. It is not their simultaneous independent
reproof or a proof from PP alone. In particular it uses no external
Li1.9728 and makes no new inference about a published Wu contraction.
