An adversarial audit of the finite compiler's actual coefficient class
10 October 2026. New proof note; no previous file or selected constant changed.

1. The narrow question and conclusion

The question is whether the signed modulus coefficient used in the count
and its exact density really has the three one-bounded convolution factors
required by the restricted PP input, after repeated labels, marked prefixes
and small-prime beta weights are accounted for. A support factorization of
each modulus would not answer that question.

The core result below is an exact orbit-count identity, including integers
with repeated prime factors. It gives a stronger scalar bound than division
by the whole group rank factorial. It also distinguishes the multiplicity
of a marked convolution from that of an ordered residual sieve prefix.
This distinction was implicit, rather than stated as a formula, in the
older insertion text. A literal replacement of every marked convolution
by a sorted indicator would be false. No such numerical replacement has
been identified in the actual inspected baseline code.

The normalization issue closes for the finite geometric families under
the explicit conditions below. This is not a new estimate for arbitrary
arithmetic weights, not a proof of the restricted distribution theorem,
and not an independent revalidation of every existing numerical integral.
I participated in the earlier compiler and lower-state audits; this note
supplies a new detailed coefficient derivation and negative controls, not
a claim of independent authorship of those old interfaces.

2. First put all interval labels on a common disjoint partition

Fix the entire finite policy, its compact margins, a fixed positive
minimum marked-prime exponent, its beta/rough parameters, and its finite
exponent meshes before T tends to infinity. Given the finitely many
original intervals, refine at every endpoint and every cutoff/ceiling
boundary. Use disjoint half-open cells I. For overlapping original
intervals, expand each indicator as the SUM of its refined cell indicators
before using a multiplicity formula. The refined alphabet size is bounded
independently of T, even if the numerical endpoints involve powers of T.
Endpoint primes are assigned to one cell, not two.

This refinement is available in the actual construction:
 - actual-k-compilation-joint-audit.tex paragraph 3 explicitly uses a
   disjoint half-open fine partition refining every coarse label;
 - own-lower-compiler-chain-audit-20261009.tex paragraph 4 uses the same
   partition while retaining all ancestor tests;
 - current upper and lower insertion texts retain these labels and a
   fixed decision on each box, with a further common refinement permitted.
Thus an original overlapping chi interval is not, by itself, an atom of
the formula below. The number of newly expanded atoms can grow with the
chosen fixed mesh, but not with T.

Let chi_I(p) be the prime indicator of cell I, with p not dividing T.
For a labelled slot list J, let m_I be the number of slots carrying I,
M(J)=product_I m_I!, and T_J=*_{j in J} chi_{I_j}.
If n has exactly m_I prime factors WITH multiplicity in each cell I,
unique factorization gives the exact unrestricted formula

 T_J(n) = product_I m_I! / product_p nu_p(n)!.                 (O1)

Otherwise T_J(n)=0. On squarefree n this is M(J). Consequently

 1_squarefree(n) T_J(n)/M(J)

is exactly the indicator of the associated sorted prime product. This
is a coefficient identity, independent of sieve densities or prime
distribution. It is false without first refining overlapping cells.

3. Any fixed whole-box grouping has an exact one-bounded normalization

Partition the slots into three groups. Let m_{I,j} be the number of
slots of label I placed in group j, and define

 M_j=product_I m_{I,j}!,
 gamma_j=(*_{slot in group j} chi_I)/M_j.

A group with no slots is delta_1. Formula (O1) gives gamma_j(n)=0 or
1/product_p nu_p(n)!, in particular 0<=gamma_j(n)<=1 on ALL integers,
not just the squarefree ones. Commutativity of convolution gives

 T_J/M(J) = c_partition (gamma_1*gamma_2*gamma_3),
 c_partition=product_j M_j/M(J)
            =product_I binomial(m_I;m_{I,1},m_{I,2},m_{I,3})^(-1)
            <=1.                                             (O2)

An existing sign or fixed scalar is retained outside (O2). The older
normalization by r_j! instead of M_j is also valid; it simply makes
the group smaller and the restoring scalar larger. It is an exact
alternative identity, not a change to a density or numerical saving.
No improvement of K follows from choosing the less wasteful normalization.

For example, for three slots in I and two in J, split as (I,J),(I,J),(I).
Then M(J)=12, all M_j=1, and c_partition=1/12. Convolution of the three
normalized groups counts twelve assignments of a squarefree product;
the final coefficient is one. Division by r_j! would instead give
scalar 1/3 and compensate it with the two group factors 1/2.

The global squarefree indicator must NOT be replaced by squarefreeness
within each group. With two I slots split one per group, the modulus
p^2 has both group factors squarefree but global squarefree coefficient
zero; the unrestricted coefficient after normalization is 1/2.
The actual insertion note explicitly removes cross-group collisions by
a separate remainder bound, rather than making this false replacement.

4. Marked convolution multiplicities are a separate colour

In a table state, sigma(b) may be an unnormalized convolution of marked
prime indicators. Its repeated-label multiplicities are already present
in sigma; they are not the same as the ordered residual sieve tuple's
multiplicities. After common refinement and off prime collisions, let
m_I count marked slots and r_I the ordered residual slots. Write
M_mark=product m_I!, M_res=product r_I!.

If sigma is the raw marked convolution, the correct combined atom is

 epsilon/M_res * 1_squarefree(T_mark*T_res) * beta,           (O3)

where the squarefree restriction is on the total rough product.
It is NOT epsilon/(M_mark*M_res) times that convolution. The marked
factor M_mark has already been counted by sigma.

The physical ceiling makes the colours particularly simple in actual
table states: residual sieve primes are strictly below the cutoff, the
cutoff is at most the retained ceiling, and all existing marked primes
are at or above that ceiling. Refine the labels at the ceiling. Their
supports are then disjoint; structural equality belongs to the marked
side and introduces no common prime. In this case M_all=M_mark*M_res.
Applying (O2) to (O3) gives a scalar bounded by |epsilon|*M_mark, a fixed
constant for a fixed finite table depth. The rank-factorial version
gives a different, still finite, scalar bound.

For a general already normalized ordered marked box, sigma equals the
marked convolution divided by M_mark, and the ordinary denominator
M_all is correct. These are two descriptions of different sigma's.
They must not be mixed in the same count/mean pair.

Concrete negative control: let marked slots both use I={11,13,17},
and residual slots both use J={19,23}. At n=11*13*19*23, the marked
convolution contributes 2, the sorted residual pair contributes 1,
and the correct total coefficient is 2. Dividing the total convolution
by M_mark*M_res instead would give 1. This diagnoses the possible
missing factor; it is not asserted to occur in the saved calculations.

The actual table estimate is linear in sigma on BOTH sides:

 Phi(sigma)=sum_b sigma(b) S(A_b;...,cutoff),
 Theta_Q(sigma)=4 li(T) sum_b sigma(b) C(bT)/[phi(b)log(Q/b)]. (O4)

Thus replacing raw sigma by sigma/M_mark on a fixed box scales Phi
and Theta by the same factor, and the H/h constants do not change.
For positive finite sums of boxes apply (O4) before summing. Do not
divide only the modulus coefficient while leaving Theta unscaled.

This is the precise reading of 'retain the original scalar' in
own-chen-table-insertion-chain-20261009.tex section 2 (sigma at line 135,
Theta at lines 139--140, original scalar at lines 159--174) and
lichtman-current-lower-density-assembly-20261009.txt section 2, line 99.
Their low-rank hypothesis is a fixed coefficient bound, not the claim
that every marked convolution has norm one.

Numerical tracing was limited but concrete: the adopted 248 baseline
adapter calls pair_cell with a diagonal flag; the author pair_cell uses
log(t_hi/t_lo)^2/2 on a tied L5 bin, and the independent pair engine
uses the same ordered-triangle mass with independently enclosed logs.
L6 uses separated rectangular labels. Thus those actual occurrences
use the ordered-prime measure, rather than silently treating an ordered
pair as an unnormalized marked convolution. The finite action recurrence
stores dimensionless ratios in (O4), not individual sigma coefficients.
This inspection and homogeneity argument resolve this interface; they
are not a fresh enumeration of all high-dimensional cell measures.
Direct code locations are certify_sharp_184_baseline.py lines 50--94
(diagonal mass at 58, the h contribution at 93), and
independent_sharp_248_baseline_replay.py lines 13--33 (table input,

certify_finite_step_table.py lines 141--142 contain normalized H/h

5. One beta pair works uniformly for every marked prefix in the family

Choose the fixed rough exponent omega smaller than the minimum marked
prime exponent in the entire finite family and all required support
reserves. At 430 generations this minimum can be very small but is
still strictly positive and chosen before T. Let w=T^omega.
All prime factors of any marked b then exceed w. Hence for p<w,

 p divides bT iff p divides T,
 g_{bT}(p)=g_T(p),                                         (O5)

including their excluded-prime universe. The same small-prime beta_T^+
and beta_T^- can therefore be constructed once for the whole family.
Their support level can be chosen below the smallest retained residual
and modulus margin. Q/b varies only in the rough support tests, which
are boxed as tests at the same complete Q. It need not, and here must
not, induce a new arbitrary beta choice for each individual b.

This is an explicit EXISTENCE completion of the analytic compiler,
not a claim that a saved numerical program already materializes this
particular beta_T or all integer coefficients rho. The density and
finite-table certificates do not construct such integer arrays. The
earlier texts permit fixed beta choices after the compact family and
its reserves are selected; (O5) now supplies a concrete uniform choice
with the required separation. Its beta-density and support reserves
must still be chosen within the already allocated compiler errors.

On a fixed atom choose the appropriate one of this pair by the fixed
rough coefficient sign. Upper majorants attach beta+ to positive and
beta- to negative coefficients; lower minorants reverse these signs.
No positivity of the beta- divisor polynomial is assumed.

For any integer n there is exactly one decomposition n=n_small*n_rough
with prime factors below w and at least w respectively. Therefore

 |(gamma_3*beta_T)(n)|
 =|gamma_3(n_rough) beta_T(n_small)|<=1.                    (O6)

Target coprimality also factors through these supports. The identity
holds when beta depends on T or the fixed atom label; it would not hold
for an arbitrary coupled coefficient beta(b,n_small). Similarly it
fails without the disjoint prime universes: if both factors are the
indicator of primes {p,q}, their convolution at pq equals 2.

6. Removing global squarefreeness is only an analytic remainder step

Let D<=T^theta, theta<1 fixed, and retain the ORIGINAL squarefree rho
in both the pointwise sieve inequality and its exact density. Form the
unrestricted convolution expansion only to estimate its discrepancy.
Its difference from rho is supported on p^2|l for p>=w. The modulus
coefficient is bounded by a fixed scalar mass (and even by one for a
single unmarked sorted policy before regrouping). Before HB expansion,
a fixed k-prime tuple weight has at most tau_k(v) representations of
a complete integer v. Uniformly this costs T^o(1). Thus the difference
in the counting term and exact coprime mean is bounded by

 C_policy T^o(1) sum_{l<=D, exists p>=w:p^2|l}
                     (T/l+1+T/phi(l))
 << C_policy (T+D) T^(-omega+o(1)).                       (O7)

For example write l=p^2 e, sum 1/e or 1/phi(e), and use
sum_{p>=w}p^(-2)<=sum_{n>=w}n^(-2)<<1/w. The count of multiples
for the +1 term is at most D*sum p^(-2). Fixed divisor powers, if
introduced by an alternative representation, only change the logarithmic
factor; they can be absorbed in the fixed-power reserve. The +1 retains
the complete product v=T without any tau(0) convention.

This proves a small discrepancy difference. It does NOT assert that the
unrestricted convolution remains a pointwise sieve majorant/minorant,
or that its density should replace the original density in K or c.

7. Uniform quantifiers and the whole-box partition

Fix the finite policy Pi, disjoint refined alphabet, maximum total rough
rank R, positive omega, finite marked multiplicities, and all margins.
For each T and each relevant HB source scale U, choose for every atom
one slot partition supplied at the ORDERED UPPER ENDPOINT vector. Its
group products bound every tuple in the full labelled box because each
prime is at most its slot's endpoint. No reassignment is made after
observing the individual prime values. Ties of labels cause no failure:
all their endpoints coincide and (O1)--(O2) include every permutation.

The exact identity (O2) or (O3) holds for every such partition, so it
may change with T, the atom, or U without changing rho. This is a
different REPRESENTATION of the same coefficient, not a new coefficient
chosen for each progression error. For fixed R there are at most 3^R
slot assignments; more importantly, the restricted convolution estimate
is uniform over all one-bounded factor sequences satisfying its stated
support bounds. No statistical independence from T is required of those
sequences. Coprimality restrictions and beta_T fall within that class.

Precisely, if the restricted source estimate is

 |E_x(gamma1,gamma2,gamma3)|<=C_{A,sigma,SW} x/log(x)^A

for every allowed three one-bounded sequences, then for a finite family
of atoms (O2)/(O3), with total absolute restoring scalar at most C_Pi,
the same expression is at most C_Pi C_{A,sigma,SW} x/log(x)^A.
C_Pi and the SW constants are independent of T. Original interval
refinement, repeated marked multiplicities, and group normalizations
are included in C_Pi. The extra HB and dyadic subdivisions cost only
a fixed logarithmic power, paid by requesting a larger A. One does not
multiply a logarithmic remainder by an N-dependent rough rank or by
an unbounded new normalization.

This use of uniformity is visible in the coefficient class of original
L23 Proposition 5.2 (saved primary text lines 946--965), and is an
explicit hypothesis of the RESTRICTED retained-phase replacement.
The original all-a theorem is not being invoked as repaired. Its four
size inequalities and the new complete-integer range remain mandatory.
Full/selected/W endpoint factorization and its buffered source exponents
are separate proved inputs, not consequences of this orbit calculation.

At low ranks retain the bounded ORIGINAL rho and apply an absolute
below-half discrepancy sum. Formula (O3) permits a finite bound C_Pi,
not necessarily one for a marked state. This note does not supply the
generalized BV theorem: its induced-character and auxiliary-coprimality
uniformity are subject to the separate low-rank proof. It only shows
exactly what coefficient norm that theorem is asked to multiply.

An arbitrary per-modulus selection can violate the conclusion. For
two disjoint prime cells, a coefficient 1_{i=j} on products p_i q_j has
matrix rank equal to the number of primes, although every product
individually factors. It cannot be represented by a uniformly bounded
number of separated tensors. Such a coupled choice is not a fixed
geometric label atom. The actual PP hypothesis excludes it. The current
common-refinement construction contains no choice based on the signs
of individual progression discrepancies.

8. Scope of this new result

Newly supplied here are (O1)--(O7), the marked/residual coloured scalar,
the prefix-uniform beta construction using (O5), the explicit coefficient
quantifiers, and negative controls for three tempting invalid shortcuts.
They justify this particular finite-family coefficient passage, provided
the cited endpoint and restricted PP inputs hold. The old text's phrase
'original factorial' must be read in the coloured sense above, or via
simultaneous normalization of sigma and Theta.

No actual arithmetic error in the inspected ordered L5/L6 baseline or
finite normalized table has been exhibited. That limited finding is not
a claim that every old numerical certificate was independently rerun.
No new K/c improvement, global restoration, formalization, publication,
or modification of a frozen file is made by this note.
