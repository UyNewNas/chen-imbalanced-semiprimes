6. Interpretation for the manuscript

The correction identifies a useful structural optimization: discard
positive cells only after paying their saved lower contribution, and
discard their matched negative cells using a restrictable density
majorant. This is more than replacing an integration limit. It gives
a concrete method improvement on the current internal route, while
retaining the distinction between numerical certificates and proof
of the analytic inputs. Neither unrestricted Lichtman large-residue
validity nor external Li1.9728 is restored. No replacement value such as1.97
is adopted as a theorem here.
