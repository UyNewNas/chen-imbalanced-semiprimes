\subsection{Whole-box integration of the existing eleven-term functional}
\label{sec:sharp-eleven-term-baseline}
This note changes neither the Chen weight nor the admissible distribution
states.  It fixes
\[
 a=417/5000,\quad h=19/125,\quad d=59/250,\quad K=67619/10000,
 \quad \nu_*=249/448,\quad w_*=25/224.
\]
The input step functions $H,h_0$ are the saved 184-generation table on the
200 closed cells $[1+i/50,1+(i+1)/50]$.  At common endpoints their value is
the smaller adjacent bound.  All its analytic state-admission hypotheses
are those in \texttt{finite-table-chen-insertion.tex}; the more recent
table changes its certified constants, not the insertion formulas.
The present arithmetic is conditional on those primitive distribution,
positive-product, finite-action and ordinary linear-sieve inputs.

Write $\bar f=f/(2e^\gamma)$, $\bar F=F/(2e^\gamma)$,
$b=s\bar f$, and $A=s\bar F$.  The ordinary delay equations imply
\[
 \bar f'={\bar F(s-1)-\bar f(s)\over s}\ge0,
 \qquad \bar F'={\bar f(s-1)-\bar F(s)\over s}\le0.
\]
Consequently $\bar f''=(\bar F'(s-1)-2\bar f')/s\le0$ on $s>2$
and $\bar F''=(\bar f'(s-1)-2\bar F')/s\ge0$ on $s>1$,
piecewise with the usual continuous limiting interpretation.  The
concavity assertion for $\bar f$ is not extended across its zero branch
$s<2$.  The existing fixed-point ordinary delay enclosure uses
trapezoids with explicit curvature errors and shape-correct interpolation;
the new arithmetic reuses that backend, not floating ordinary functions.

\paragraph{Two-prefix lower integrals.}
For $R=V(t,u)-t-u$, cancel the dependent occurrence of $R$ before bounding:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sharp-pair-cancellation}
 {4b(R/a)\over tuR}={4\over a tu}\bar f(R/a).
\end{equation}
Here $V$ is exactly the existing maximum of the admitted caps
$v(t),v(t+u),153/256-3a/8$ on the enhanced part, and is $v(t)$
on the old nonenhanced part.  On a rectangle choose one of these caps.
A tent $\min(\ell_1,\ell_2)$ is replaced by $\ell_i$ only if the
affine inequality $\ell_i\le\ell_{3-i}$ holds throughout the rectangle.
Thus a retained affine residual $r=c+\beta t+\delta u$ satisfies
$r\le R$ everywhere.  If $r_-\ge2a$, concavity and monotonicity give
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sharp-pair-chord}
 \int_B\bar f(R/a)d\mu_2\ge
 { f_-\{r_+M-M_r\}+f_+\{M_r-r_-M\}\over r_+-r_-},
 \quad d\mu_2={dt\,du\over tu},
\end{equation}
where $M=\int_Bd\mu_2$, $M_r=\int_Br,d\mu_2$, and $f_\pm$ are
proved lower bounds for $\bar f(r_\pm/a)$.  The two coefficients are
nonnegative.  Each admissible affine candidate and the constant bound
$M\bar f(R_-/a)$ yields a lower bound; their maximum, not their sum,
is used for the box.  If $r_-<2a$, that chord is not used.

These moments require only logarithms.  For a rectangle $[t_0,t_1]
\times[u_0,u_1]$, writing $L_t=\log(t_1/t_0)$ and
$L_u=\log(u_1/u_0)$, one has
\[
 M=L_tL_u,\quad M_t=(t_1-t_0)L_u,\quad M_u=(u_1-u_0)L_t.
\]
On the diagonal triangle $l<u<t<v$, instead
\[
 M=\tfrac12\log^2(v/l),\quad
 M_t=v\log(v/l)-(v-l),\quad M_u=(v-l)-l\log(v/l).
\]
Thus no unjustified half-rectangle average is used for a nonsymmetric
affine integrand.  The table contribution is separately bounded below
by $4h_BM/R_+$, where $h_B$ is the minimum of every closed cell meeting
the whole actual interval $[R_-/a,R_+/a]$.  It is zero when that interval
is not wholly in the certified lower-table domain.  The previously
proved $a\ge25/352$ ensures the required residual $\alpha\ge2$ on the
enhanced branches; the original full-square and ceiling gates remain.

\paragraph{One-prefix upper integrals.}
On $R=A_0-B_0t\le3a$, including the cutoff-clamped region, $A=1$.
The ordinary cost is integrated exactly as
\[
 4\int{dt\over tR}={4\over A_0}\log{t\over A_0-B_0t}.
\]
For $R>3a$ the integrand is $4\bar F(R/a)/(at)$.  A convex endpoint
chord is an upper bound and is integrated using $\int dt/t$ and
$\int (R/a)dt/t$.  The $H$ correction is split exactly at rational
inverse images of the table knots, including the positive-measure
clamped region.  The ordinary starting terms $L_1,L_2$ are evaluated
by the ordinary delay enclosure.  The three older switching costs
$U_7,U_8,U_9$ use $K=6.7619$ and rigorous one-dimensional enclosures.

\paragraph{A complete ordered cover for the last two costs.}
The domains remain
\[
 \Omega_{10}=\{a<w<z<y<x<h\},\qquad
 \Omega_{11}=\{a<w<z<y<h<x<d\},\quad
 d\mu_4={dx\,dy\,dz\,dw\over xyzw}.
\]
Uniform bins are assigned in weakly decreasing index order.  Equal
indices specify the strictly ordered simplex inside that bin.  This
partitions the full domain except null boundaries; they are neither
discarded nor counted as whole rectangles.  A block of $m$ equal
indices in $[l,u]$ has log mass $L^m/m!$, $L=\log(u/l)$.
If the coordinate $z$ is the $(r+1)$st largest in that block, and $s$
coordinates of the block follow it, its additional $1/z$ moment is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sharp-tied-bin-moment}
 {1\over l r!s!}\int_0^L e^{-t}(L-t)^r t^s\,dt,
 \qquad r+s=m-1.
\end{equation}
Expand only this polynomial; every moment is exact in terms of
$J_j=j![1-(l/u)\sum_{k=0}^jL^k/k!]$.  Directed arithmetic accounts
for its alternating expansion.  All other blocks contribute their
ordinary log mass.

The switching kernel is the actual positive-product kernel
$K\omega((1-S)/z)/z$, $S=x+y+z+w$.  Its argument is at least
$(1-d-3h)/h>2$ throughout these domains; $S\le d+3h<1$ excludes
the unit residual on the main product scale.  No moving-$\alpha$
argument is replaced by $2$ in this step.  For a rigorous upper
enclosure of $\omega$, set $U(s)=s\omega(s)$, $U=1$ on $[1,2]$,
$U'=\omega(s-1)$ above two.  Positivity and the delay equation give
$0\le\omega\le1$, $|\omega'|\le1/s$ above two, and piecewise
$|\omega''|\le2$ on $[1,\infty)$.  The numerical grid contains all
integer breaks.  Trapezoids have integral error at most $\Delta^3/6$;
inside a cell, $\omega$ is at most the larger upper endpoint bound
plus $\Delta^2/4$.  Taking the maximum over all met cells, and the
minimum with the already proved $C=70893/125000$, gives a uniform
box upper bound $\omega_B$.

The ordinary alternative uses one of the fifteen selected-subset
branches or the existing $W$ branch, with every full-square, remaining-
selected-square and $W$ gate checked as an exact affine whole-box
inequality.  These are precisely the tests in
\texttt{four-prefix-safe-minimum.tex}.  The parameter bounds give
$s=\max(2,R/w)\le1249/417<3$ and $\alpha\ge2$, so its cost is
$4(1-H(s))/R$.  On a strict rectangle, $1/R=\max_j1/r_j$ is
separately convex; its multilinear vertex interpolation is integrated
with the positive affine-basis moments against $d\mu_4$.  On a tied-bin
simplex the deliberately coarser bound $4(1-H_B)/R_-$ times its exact
log mass suffices.  Repeated exponent bins can be subdivided before
constructing actual prime atoms; the equality faces have zero mass.
Pointwise cap endpoints and table discontinuities are realized by a
fixed finite refinement with margins before taking $N\to\infty$,
as in the existing endpoint-integral interface.  No individual-prefix
distribution estimate is inferred from its averaged version.

For each ordered box let $B_B$ be the exact integral of $KC/z$,
$O_B$ an ordinary upper bound when its gates pass, and
$W_B=K\omega_B\int_Bd\mu_4/z$.  The saving is bounded below by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sharp-four-min-saving}
 \max\{0,(B_B)_--(O_B)_+,(B_B)_--(W_B)_+\}.
\end{equation}
The two savings are not added.  Ineligible ordinary candidates leave
the switched option available.  The whole uniform $KC/z$ integrals
also have exact log-moment formulas, using \eqref{eq:sharp-tied-bin-moment}.

\paragraph{Replacement arithmetic and scope.}
The first new baseline keeps the old $U_{10},U_{11}$ reference
$K=6.7672$ and its single old four-prefix patch.  The second certificate
replaces that entire effective cost by the new uniform cost at $6.7619$
minus the saving \eqref{eq:sharp-four-min-saving}.  It never adds a new
patch to an old one.  With the frozen 184 table, $128$ pair divisions,
$4096$ ordinary mesh and $32$ four-prime bins, the exact lower bound is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sharp-184-baseline-value}
 c_{184}\ge
 {9369578785614097433419615\over4835703278458516698824704}
 >1.93758347.
\end{equation}
It replaces the old complete baseline $1.9304185967\ldots$.
No third-, fourth- or fifth-prefix lower-start improvement is included.
A separate new starting policy must still change the ordinary starting
term and remove its corresponding $H$ range exactly once.  In particular
an expanded $H$ bank requires its own range-removal integral; an old
smaller removal cannot be reused without a proof of domination.
The second implementation verifies every stored new four-prime floor
using 112-bit arithmetic, rational logarithms and a finer independently
built Buchstab table.  That is an alternate implementation, not an
independent second-person proof of the analytic inputs or of the entire
baseline.  The separate reviewer records its own scope.
