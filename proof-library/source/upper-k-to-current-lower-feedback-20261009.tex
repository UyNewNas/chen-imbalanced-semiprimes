\paragraph{Precisely the new input.}

\paragraph{Only the saved switched cells of the last two costs.}
Retain exactly the 1,011,131 unchanged $n=48$ method-zero cells of

\[

\begin{align*}
 KT_B&\le {K\over K_0}A_B
 \le A_B-(K_0-K)s_B\\

                    -(K_*-K)s_B.

any mass or replacing its method. It correctly retains the effect of

\paragraph{Finite choices and an explicit budget-safe conclusion.}
The hypotheses on $K$ must be proved on the upper side with their own
strict endpoint slack. If an endpoint $E<K$ is available, its density
and compilation losses must together fit inside $K-E$. The old upper
budget numbers cannot simply be copied after changing this endpoint.
Once the actual uniform theorem with coefficient $K$ has been proved,
these upper losses are already included in $K$ and are not charged
again to the lower coefficient.

On the lower side the same finite marked choices and the same retained
cells are used. Further common refinement preserves these decisions;
it does not change a cap, expand a domain, or introduce a new table.
The inherited fixed-family interface permits all additional smoothing
and compilation errors to be made arbitrarily small before the
eventual even-$N$ limit. The old budgets $L_a,L_b$ are retained in full.
For a completely explicit conservative implementation, do not assume
that an unrecorded new error is zero. The current unused lower slack is
\[
 \sigma=C_*-L_a-L_b-c_*
 ={11064375335890447617827243029254136337\over
 10971810039914578419175113477672927232000000}>10^{-6}.
\]
Allow an extra $\sigma/4$ for finite approximation and another
$\sigma/4$ for compilation. The finite union of the unchanged families
and the new upper strategy can be realized within these bounds, under
the same arbitrarily-small-error interface. The resulting lower
coefficient is at least
\[
 C_*+A(K_*-K)-L_a-L_b-\sigma/2
 =c_*+A(K_*-K)+\sigma/2.
\]
In particular the displayed own-route value $c_*$ can be retained, and
even $c_*+A(K_*-K)$ has strict remaining slack. The raw slope in
\eqref{eq:current-lower-fixed-affine-feedback} does not require a new
large integral. This paragraph supplies a sufficient finite budget;
it makes no claim that extra losses of this size are necessary.
No value of $K$, new imbalance exponent, global state, external lower
theorem, or optimality claim is selected here.
