1. Independent derivation of the recurrence enclosure

Let L=s*f0 and H=s*F0 with L'=H(s-1)/(s-1) and
H'=L(s-1)/(s-1), on their respective nonconstant ranges. With
a=j/n, b=(j+1)/n, direct integration of the affine endpoint interpolant
of a numerator V gives
  integral_a^b chord(V)(u)/u du = w0 V(a)+w1 V(b),
  w0=(j+1)log(1+1/j)-1,
  w1=1-j log(1+1/j).
The weights are integrals of nonnegative linear basis functions divided
by u>0, hence positive. They are not trapezoid weights for V/u.

The standard monotonicities give L''=F0'(s-1)<=0 and H''=f0'(s-1)>=0.
From F0<=1, 0<=f0'<=1/s and |F0'|<=1/s follow from their equations;
thus the absolute numerator curvature on a delayed cell is <=1/(a-1).
Integrating |V-chord(V)|<=(M/2)(u-a)(b-u) against 1/u<=1/a gives
  error <= M*(b-a)^3/(12a)=1/[12*n*j*(j-n)].
For a convex H its chord is an upper estimate, so only the lower L
increment loses this error. For a concave L the chord is a lower
estimate, so only the upper H increment gains it. The code has these
directions. The exact H=1 range permits the logarithmic L update through
s=4. H first updates strictly after s=3, where j>n and the denominator
is positive. Both delayed indices precede the target index.

Expanding log(1+1/j) independently gives the two alternating weight
series printed in the producer report. Their fourth partial sums,
with denominator 60*j^5, have numerators
  30*j^4-10*j^3+5*j^2-3*j,
  30*j^4-20*j^3+15*j^2-12*j.
The next positive terms add respectively 2 and 10. The logarithm's
fourth numerator is 60*j^4-30*j^3+20*j^2-15*j and its next term adds12.
These agree with the code. Alternating terms decrease for j>=1.
The fourth sums are positive, so multiplication by nonnegative endpoint
intervals preserves direction. All floors/ceilings use positive divisors.
The monotone maximum on the lower L endpoint remains a lower bound.

For interpolation, a chord error <=M*h^2/8 with M<=1/(i/n-1)
gives 1/[8*n*(i-n)], exactly the independent implementation's bound.
At a knot the code returns the knot and does not access its successor.
No grid cell crosses the initial derivative break at2 or an integer
delay break. The extension beyond the last lower-function knot uses
f0(max)<=f0(s)<=F0(max), and makes no analogous unsupported H extension.
The final 197 H queries are additional comparisons, not the justification
for this general interpolation argument.
