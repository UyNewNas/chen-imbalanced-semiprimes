\subsection{Independent audit of the disjoint new fifth-prefix cover}

All $43236$ new grid-$400$ boxes and all $1720184$ new grid-$800$
boxes have been independently reconstructed. The replay imports neither
author program nor the older rational logarithm helper. It checks
every geometry and every saved floor, not a sample of the boxes.
Its total is $1763420$ boxes in $94$ blocks. The completed replay is
\texttt{fifth-new-coverage-independent-replay.json}; the execution
finished normally in approximately $34.9$ seconds.

\paragraph{Whole-box admission and the actual previous tree.}
The coordinates have strictly ordered interiors, $t_5\ge1/50$, and
$t_1\le39/200<\mu=25/128$. The original first-prime stop is therefore
respected. On every box the replay finds one original third component
whose minimum at the $32$ vertices exceeds $t_1+t_2+3t_3$.
Each such component is affine or the minimum of two affine functions,
so its gap from the linear third test is concave and the vertex minimum
is a valid whole-box minimum. Taking the maximum of these component
minima is legitimate. Merely taking the minimum of a vertexwise maximum
would not prove the same claim and is not used.

The new selected mask is one of $17,\ldots,31$. For its endpoint
$M=\min\{(1+s_I)/2,57/64-3s_I/2\}$ the replay checks both defining
planes against $1/2$, all five full square tests, and all remaining
selected square tests at every vertex. Concavity again extends the
positive minima to the entire box. The independent minimum full/selected
slacks are $8$ at grid $400$ and $16$ at grid $800$, in the stated
$32n$ affine scale. Minimum old-third margins are $16$ in both grids.
The center mask choice is not used as evidence of any of these claims.

For every raw four-slot cap the replay compares its two doubled
midpoint plane values, freezes the lower choice on this numerical box,
and then evaluates that chosen affine function at all vertices.
Either plane is globally an upper bound for its raw minimum, regardless
of the midpoint choice. Including the inherited $W_3$ plane gives
$U\ge R_4\ge L_4$ for the actual allowed fourth tree. A different
choice on a fine child is only a different majorant for that same
fourth tree. It does not change the fourth endpoint or its saving.

The full fifth endpoint is at least every admitted $M$ and at least
$L_4$. Its density saving is nonnegative. On these new cells the same
full fifth tree is used as in the old parent certificate. The sixth
certificate remains compatible: its old membership is forced by the
unchanged third cap, and its raw first-five envelope already dominates
every selected fifth endpoint, including those whose gains are newly
certified here. No new sixth increment is inferred by this audit.

\paragraph{The ratio and Jensen calculations are reconstructed.}
Set $S_5=\sum_jt_j$ and
\[
 d=M-\max\{U,S_5+2t_5\}.
\]
This is concave. Its $32$ vertex values are summed in units $1/(32n)$,
giving the saved integer $d_\Sigma$. For each of the two planes defining
$M$, the independent program maximizes $(\text{plane}-S_5)/t_5$
over all $32$ vertices using exact cross products; it then takes the
smaller of those two maxima. It does not eliminate the first four
coordinates as the author does. A linear-fractional function with a
positive denominator has its extrema at vertices here, so the result
$b=A/B$ bounds $(M-S_5)/t_5$ throughout the box. Tied unreduced pairs
are compared as rational numbers, not by their raw numerators.

Every reconstructed ratio matches the saved ratio and satisfies
\[
 2<b\le4,\qquad e=\frac{d_\Sigma}{1024h_5}>0,
 \qquad e\le b-2.
\]
The minimum integer convexity-domain slacks are $1622016$ and $2859520$.
Let $f_0(s)=\log(s-1)/s$ on $[2,4]$, with zero extension below two.
On $[2,4]$ it is increasing and concave: with $x=s-1\in[1,3]$, the
sign expressions are $1+1/x-\log x>0$ and
$3+4/x+1/x^2-2\log x>0$. Both decrease in $x$, and their endpoint
signs follow from $\log3<4/3$. Thus
$Q_b(e)=f_0(b)-f_0(b-e)$ is increasing and convex on $[0,b-2]$.

The positive part $d_+$ is introduced before pulling out the positive
density lower bound. Pointwise, the saving from the actual fourth cap
to an admitted fifth cap dominates $Q_b(d_+/(h_5/n))$.
Furthermore $0\le d_+/(h_5/n)\le b-2$. Concavity of $d$ gives
$\mathbb E d_+\ge\mathbb E d\ge d_\Sigma/(1024n)$.
Jensen's inequality and monotonicity now give the box lower bound
\[
 \frac{4n}{(\prod_i h_i)h_5}Q_b(e).
\]
The factor $n$ is the actual grid, $400$ or $800$: the box volume
$n^{-5}$ cancels the $n^5$ from the reciprocal-prime density, leaving
the additional $n/h_5$ from $1/t_5$. Negative individual vertex gaps
are allowed and have not been silently dropped from their mean.

\paragraph{Independent directed logarithms.}
Put $a=b-e$, $z=e/[2(b-1)-e]$, and $y=(a-2)/a$. The replay uses
\[
 L_6(z)=2\sum_{j=0}^{5}\frac{z^{2j+1}}{2j+1},\qquad
 U_7(y)=2\sum_{j=0}^{6}\frac{y^{2j+1}}{2j+1}
       +\frac{2y^{15}}{15(1-y^2)}.
\]
These respectively bound $\log((b-1)/(a-1))$ from below and
$\log(a-1)$ from above. Hence a lower bound for $Q_b(e)$ is
$(aL_6-eU_7)/(ab)$, with the subtraction direction retained.
The implementation derives a positive homogeneous Horner polynomial
using the least common multiple of the odd denominators. It uses only
arbitrary-precision integers, independently of the old four/five-term
engine. Fifteen exact Fraction boundary checks, including $a=2$, and
$549$ actual-box Fraction checks give identical floors. Every one of
the $1763420$ full replay floors is at least its stored author floor.
