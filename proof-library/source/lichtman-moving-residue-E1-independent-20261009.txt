Independent audit of the near-linear-residue E1 replacement
2026-10-09

Conclusion: the supplied E1 replacement follows in the stated subclass
|a|>=c*x, theta=7/32, from the direct Weil argument of Maynard I v2
Lemma17.2 and B1-B3. It does not establish the full all-a statement of
L23 Lemma4.7, the spectral E2 estimate, or the preliminary dispersion.
No global K/c/b is restored by this audit.

1. Source and uniformity.
Read the actual Maynard I v2 Lemma17.2, local text lines6445-6570,
including (17.7)-(17.14), rather than the older reference number17.3.
The coefficients lambda(q,r) may depend arbitrarily on q and r and need
only be bounded. A fixed d therefore permits q'=d*q and a coefficient
1_{d divides q'} Lambda(d,r). This permission is absent in Lemma5.1;
the same substitution there would not follow from this audit.

After Cauchy, the completed n sum has modulus q*r1*r1prime and phase
frequency a*(h*r1prime-hprime*r1). Retaining the original coprimality
(a,q*r1*r1prime)=1 makes its gcd factor exactly independent of a.
No continuous differentiation of this reciprocal phase is used.
The ell=0 count and subsequent positive gcd sums are independent of a.
The source's classical completed inverse-phase estimate then applies
uniformly; the underlying Weil estimate remains an external theorem.

There is an isolated extra condition (r1,r2)=1 in the source's displayed
first Cauchy step, although its statement does not impose it. Do not add
that restriction to the original complex sum. Derive Cauchy with the
correct restriction (r1,a*n1)=1. The bounded coefficient may depend on
r2. Upon expanding and taking the completed n sum, r2 has disappeared
from the phase and all later bounds use the same modulus q*r1*r1prime.
They require no coprimality of r1 and r2. Thus this correct version has
the same final bound. The author supplement now explicitly records this
point. The printed n1 in a completed sum over n is a variable-name typo.

2. Exact size combinations.
Let n,q,u,v denote logarithmic lengths of N,Q1,Q2,Q3. With theta=7/32
and |a|>=c*x, the third monomial condition gives
 n+q+5u+2v <= 2-theta-15sigma+O(1/log x).
The exact combination
 (35/16)(q-n)+(23/16)(2n+u+2v)+(5/16)(n+q+5u+2v)-v/2
 = n+(5/2)q+3u+3v
has upper bound 1021/512-(147/8)sigma+O(1/log x).
The discarded v/2 is nonnegative. The margin below2 is 3/512 before
the additional sigma saving. Independently n+q<1-9sigma and
2n+q+u+v<17/10-(29/2)sigma. All three dominate the needed4sigma margin.
These identities are independently checked with exact fractions.

3. Fixed d, Fourier length and normalization.
Set L=d*Q1 and rough length R, D*R comparable to Q2*Q3. The actual
E1 denominator phi(q*d*r2)*q*d*r1 becomes precisely the source one.
The smooth q weight is nonnegative and may be absorbed by its square
root into lambda, after a bounded normalization. Splitting fixed smooth
supports into a finite number of dyadic intervals is harmless.
H_actual/(L*R/M*log^5 x)=D/d is bounded. The proof already allows bounded
coefficients in h and its estimates remain valid for fixed multiples
of the endpoint; strict power margins absorb every such constant.

The three size products are, up to constants/subpower factors,
 NL=NQ1*x^o(1),
 NL^(5/2)R^3 <= NQ1^(5/2)(Q2Q3)^3 D^(-1/2),
 N^2LR <= N^2Q1Q2Q3.
Thus epsilon_W=sigma is permissible. Normalize Lambda's fixed divisor
bound at an x^o(1) cost after this power estimate. For each d the bound
is N^2/(dQ1)*x^(-sigma+o(1)). Sum 1/d over its dyadic range, then pay
the one remaining D=x^o(1) normalization to obtain
 |E1| << N^2/(DQ1)*x^(-sigma/2).
No multiplication of a merely logarithmic remainder by x^o(1) occurs.

4. Limits of the finding.
The lower bound on |a| is used in the exact second Weil-size inequality
and cannot be removed from this proof. B4 and the spectral theorem are
not used here. This supplies the E1 input for the project's near-linear
residue subclass conditional on its B1-B3 parameters and coefficient
scope, not the original unrestricted theorem or the full Chen chain.
The independently reviewed root preliminary repair and separately
audited E2 input must still be combined before any repaired proposition
