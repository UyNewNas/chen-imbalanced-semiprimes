2026-10-10. Scope: one separated medium block of the restricted PP route.

Finding

No counterexample or unfilled coefficient hypothesis was found for the
actual individual medium factor described below. Its full auxiliary SW
property follows from classical unrough Mobius SW by explicit finite
identities, uniformly in the original chart, target, interval, and allowed
Mellin frequency. There is a useful simplification: the actual alpha is
already one-bounded. Truncate only the complementary beta. This removes
Maynard Lemma13.7 / L23 Lemma4.3 from this particular insertion, without
claiming that their general statements are false.

The resulting block estimate is conditional on the separately stated
restricted one-bounded convolution estimate and the fixed tensor modulus

spectral or preliminary-dispersion components, establish the coupled
Mellin decomposition, or validate all PP, K, c, b. Those dependencies are
identified explicitly below. In particular a status label is not used as
a substitute for any of them.

1. The actual block, before invoking a distribution estimate

Fix the outer positive cutoff, maximum original prime count k_star, one
finite policy, and its positive support margins. On an original chart
p_i in [R_i,2R_i], set X=product R_i and Xplus=2^k X. Fix

  Zchart=f(C_k Xplus),  f(v)=v^(1/(log log v)^3),
  C_k=2^(6 k_star+2),

BEFORE the order-three HB expansion. All individual variables in every
HB summand have this same rough projection. Original group labels remain
fixed. The medium index is selected deterministically from its dyadic
lengths on this chart, before the values of integers or modulus errors
are known; it is not selected separately at each integer.

The source x=UV is the COMPLETE product scale after grouping the other
variables. On retained rectangles x is within fixed factors of X and
x<=C_k Xplus. Hence, uniformly on those rectangles, eventually

  f(x)<=Zchart<=x^(2/(log log x)^3),
  x^(2 zeta)<=U<=x^(1/3+zeta/2).

The medium slot comes from one of the factors mu_{(2R_i)^(1/3)}, 1, log
in the exact groupwise HB identity. After allowed separation and log
normalization its coefficient has the form

  alpha_n=e(n) 1_{Pminus(n)>Zchart} 1_I(n) h(n) n^(it),       (M1)
  e=1 or mu, I subset [U,2U], |h|<=1,
  Var h<=C_h (log x)^B, |t|<=(log x)^B,

where B,C_h are fixed before x tends to infinity. A truncated mu factor
merely changes I. The modulus does not enter (M1). Coprimality with that
modulus belongs in the discrepancy, not in an unlicensed alpha filter.
Units cannot be medium. Fixed HB coefficients and omitted logarithmic
normalizations are outside this sequence and have fixed/polylog cost.

If there are J complementary slots, J<=6 k_star-1, their normalized
convolution satisfies |beta_m|<=d_J(m)<=tau(m)^(J-1). Indeed, for p^a
the number of weak J-part compositions is at most (a+1)^(J-1).
Subintervals and unit-modulus twists do not increase this bound. Thus
the divisor order B_beta is fixed by k_star, not by the desired saving,
target, SW exponent, or Mellin frequency. For example, a two-original-
prime, twelve-slot HB term has J<=11 and B_beta<=10.

This deduction assumes a genuinely separated summand. Whether a coupled
original group cutoff has been separated with the required total norm is
the separate HB/Mellin obligation being audited in the parallel report;
this note does not silently discard that cutoff.

2. Classical input and the precise uniform SW statement

The external input is classical unrough Mobius Siegel-Walfisz: for every
fixed A0,B0, q<=log(V)^B0, primitive b mod q, and any interval J in
[V,2V], the primitive-class discrepancy of mu is O(V log(V)^(-A0)),
uniform in q,b,J. Constants may be ineffective. Endpoint subtraction
supplies arbitrary subintervals. Maynard I v2 Definition1, (6.1), and
the examples immediately after it explicitly record even the full
auxiliary SW property of unrough mu; only its auxiliary-free special
case is used here. This is not the disputed large-residue proposition.

For fixed xi>0, 0<c1<=c2, let x^xi<=U<=x and
x^(c1/(log log x)^3)<=Z<=x^(c2/(log log x)^3). Then (M1) satisfies,
for EVERY d,q>=1 and primitive b mod q,

 |sum_{n=b mod q,(n,d)=1} alpha_n
  -phi(q)^(-1) sum_{(n,dq)=1} alpha_n|
       <=C_A U tau(d) (log x)^(-A).                         (M2)

Constants are uniform in Z, d, q, b, interval endpoints, target-dependent
choices within this window and the stipulated h,t. No limit xi->0,
c1->0 or infinite policy is allowed. The divisor exponent on d is 1
and does not grow with A. This distinction is needed by the source's
SW condition and by its subsequent logarithmic truncations.

3. Explicit derivation of (M2), including the shortest inner scale

Write L2=log log x, m=2 floor(L2^2), k=m+1 and L=U^(1/4).
For sufficiently large x, uniformly in Z, Z^k<=L. The even and odd
Brun polynomials rho^+,rho^- summing squarefree small-prime divisors
with at most m,m+1 factors satisfy

 rho^-<=1_{Pminus>Z}<=rho^+,
 rho^+-rho^-=binom(omega(gcd(n,product_{p<=Z}p)),k)>=0.

There are at most L possible supported divisors. On a primitive
progression, divisors sharing a prime with q disappear exactly. The
sum of the gap, and its primitive-class average, are at most

  (U/q) H(Z)^k/k! + O(L),  H(Z)=sum_{p<=Z}1/p.

The elementary Euler product bound H(Z)<=e log(1+log Z)=O(L2) gives
H(Z)^k/k!<=exp(-c L2^2 log L2), smaller than every log power.
For mu the replacement error is bounded ABSOLUTELY by this nonnegative
gap, since |mu 1_rough-mu rho^+|<=rho^+-rho^-.

For a squarefree divisor ell in the expansion,
mu(ell v)=mu(ell)mu(v)1_{(v,ell)=1}. The auxiliary restriction is
handled by the exact primewise identity

 mu(v)1_{(v,E)=1}=sum_{w|v, p|w implies p|E} mu(v/w).        (M3)

On scale V split w at V^(1/4). For small w, (w,q)>1 makes both
primitive expressions zero; otherwise change b to b/w mod q and apply
classical SW. The harmonic sum over w supported on E is
product_{p|E}(1-1/p)^(-1)<=tau(E). The large-w mass is bounded by

  2V sum_{w>V^(1/4),w|E^infinity}1/w
  <=2 V^(7/8) product_{p|E}(1-p^(-1/2))^(-1)
  <=2 V^(7/8) tau(E)^2.

The counting bound floor(2V/w)<=2V/w is essential: no uncontrolled
sum of ones is added. In the Brun expansion V=U/ell>=U^(3/4), so the
shortest scale in a classical SW use is V/w>=V^(3/4)>=U^(9/16).
Consequently every fixed q<=log(x)^B0 is in a fixed logarithmic range
of that inner scale; enlarge its logarithmic exponent once if needed.
This is uniform, including at chart, interval and HB-cutoff endpoints.

After summing all ell<=L, the errors are bounded by

 U log(x)^(-A0) sum_{ell<=L}tau(ell)/ell
       << U log(x)^(2-A0),
 U^(7/8) sum_{ell<=L}tau(ell)^2/ell^(7/8)
       << U^(29/32)(1+log U)^4.

The growing Brun order causes no growing divisor exponent here.
For e=1 the expanded progression counts have O(1) discrepancy each.
These arguments prove auxiliary-free rough SW for small q. For large
q use one-boundedness and phi(q)>>sqrt(q): the discrepancy is
O(U/sqrt(q)+1). A sufficiently large fixed logarithmic threshold for
q proves the assertion for every modulus, even q beyond the support.

Now insert any auxiliary d. Only prime divisors p|d with p>Z can
remove a rough integer. The change in either counting expression is
at most the total removed mass; the change in the discrepancy is

 <=2 sum_{p|d,p>Z}(U/p+1)
 <=2(U/Z+1)omega(d)<<_A U tau(d)log(x)^(-A).

This is a sum over PRIME divisors, works for arbitrarily large d, and
does not use d<=x^constant. Finally Stieltjes partial summation costs
at most 1+Var h+|t|log2=O(log(x)^B). Start with A+B+3 in the rough
estimate. This proves (M2), including the exact coprime mean, without
asserting positivity for a signed mu sum.

4. Target and selection changes: what is and is not uniform

Original chart constants depend only on the fixed k_star. The cutoff
window has c1=1,c2=2, and the medium length has xi=2 zeta (or any
fixed smaller value). The estimate above therefore permits a target-
dependent chart, interval and fixed-polylog twist. Deterministic choice
among finitely many HB slot types, or logarithmically many charts,
does not alter a common constant. A stronger requested log saving pays
their count and their separated total norm.

An extra arbitrary target-dependent arithmetic weight is NOT permitted.
For example 1_{n=1 mod 3} is not covered by this argument. A choice of
the medium slot depending on n or on signs of modulus errors is likewise
not covered. The project's stated choice depends only on fixed length
charts; the original group cutoff is retained until valid separation.
This is the exact boundary of the conclusion, not an all-coefficient
SW stability principle.

5. Beta-only normalization: no SW truncation lemma is needed here

Put Lx=log x and beta'=beta*1_{tau(m)<=Lx^C}, for a fixed C chosen
below. Leave alpha completely unchanged. Then beta'/Lx^(B_beta C)
is one-bounded and (M2) remains literally the same statement.

For b=nm the absolute deleted coefficient is bounded by

 sum_{nm=b}|alpha_n beta_m|1_{tau(m)>Lx^C}
       <= Lx^(-C) tau(b)^(B_beta+2).                       (M4)

If the original three-factor modulus coefficient is grouped as rho_l,
its fixed divisor majorant gives, for b!=T,
sum_{l|b-T}|rho_l|<=tau(|b-T|)^(J0+1), with fixed J0. Thus the deleted
counting term is at most

 Lx^(-C) sum_{b~x,b!=T}tau(b)^(B_beta+2)tau(|b-T|)^(J0+1)
       << x Lx^(E-C),                                    (M5)

where E depends on fixed divisor orders and a fixed polynomial range
for T, not on C. This is the q=1 case of the shifted-moment bound.
For this case alone it can be seen directly by Cauchy: use standard
divisor moments for b, and classical Shiu for the nonzero interval
|b-T|. Here b~x and T<=x^(1+kappa), with kappa<1; each relevant large
interval of length comparable to x has length at least its location
to a fixed power <1/2. For small |b-T| use ordinary dyadic divisor
moments. Shiu's modulus is 1, so there is no primitive/nonprimitive
residue issue in this use. The quoted primitive Shiu hypotheses are
not being extended to an unsupported moving shift.

The exact b=T is kept separate. Its full coefficient mass is at most
tau(T)^(B_beta+1), and its modulus mass is
Dtot log(x)^O(1). Hence it is Dtot*x^o(1). The restricted four scale
conditions give Dtot<<x^(7/10-13sigma/2); fixed constants can be
absorbed to get, for example, Dtot<=x^(4/5) eventually. Therefore the
exact-product term is x^(4/5+o(1)), before any optional use of rough
parity. It has a fixed power margin and causes no tau(0).

For the removed coprime mean, sum (M4) over b and use
sum_{l<=Dtot}|rho_l|/phi(l)<<log(x)^E1. Dropping its coprime indicator
only increases the absolute bound. This gives x log(x)^(E2-C), again
with E2 independent of C.

Fix the desired block exponent A and an extra fixed logarithmic cost K
for recombination. First choose C>A+K+max(E,E2)+3. Request the one-
bounded proposition with A0=A+K+B_beta*C+3 and multiply its conclusion
back by log(x)^(B_beta*C). Then the retained and deleted terms are
O(x log(x)^(-A-K)). Only the exact-product term uses x^o(1), and it
has the fixed power saving just proved. There is no fixed-point choice
of C and A0 and no x^o(1) multiplier on a log-saving remainder.

The one-bounded proposition still sees its ORIGINAL separate bounded
gamma(q1),lambda(q2),nu(q3). The grouped rho majorant was used only
for deleted tails; it does not license arbitrary rho in the retained
convolution theorem. Beta needs no SW assertion. This simpler actual-
coefficient argument does not require any claim about SW after a cutoff
in |alpha| or tau(alpha's argument).

Use N=U, M=V, a=T, theta=7/32, and the fixed tensor assignment of the

 Q1<U x^(-sigma), U^2 Q2 Q3^2<x^(1-8sigma),

These are exactly the original four monomials in the restricted

  c0 x<=T<=x^(1+kappa).

The scale is the complete product, NOT the residual quotient after

For completeness, the residual proof's parameter normalization is

7. Dependencies and the first obligations outside this audit

The coefficient hypothesis is derived, not reintroduced as an unnamed
assumption. Its external distribution input is classical unrough mu SW.
The beta-tail bound additionally invokes classical Shiu for a fixed
divisor power, or the already explicit shifted-moment proof. Neither
input is the desired beyond-half convolution conclusion.

For the final BLOCK remainder one must separately supply the restricted
one-bounded convolution theorem, whose inputs are preliminary dispersion,
Weil/Poisson, SW mean square, and the stated classical spectral estimates;
and the fixed tensor modulus assignment proving (M6). This audit has
read their stated parameters and normalization but has NOT re-proved
their complete analytic proofs. The current classical spectral
substitution is therefore still an external component of this block's
derivation, not a consequence of this report.

For recombination one must also prove that the original coupled HB
constraints yield separated summands with the stipulated fixed-polylog
norm and error, with the original rough projector unchanged. That
separate task cannot be replaced by (M2). The no-medium long alternatives,
finite sieve compiler, raw densities, Chen insertion and numeric ledger
are outside scope. Failure of the restricted convolution theorem, its
modulus assignment or that separation would still invalidate the
high-rank medium contribution to PP and hence the present beyond-half
K/c route. Closing this coefficient block alone does not repair them.
